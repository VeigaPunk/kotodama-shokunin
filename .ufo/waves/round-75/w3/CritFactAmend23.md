## Findings / impacted axes / smallest safe correction

1. **Todorov adoption overclaim — calibration / attribution.** The quote “Les formalistes russes voyaient…” reports what the Russian Formalists saw; it does not itself establish that Todorov adopts their thesis. The adjacent page presents Vygotsky's passage and describes formalist theory, but the amendment's statement “he adopts it” and “Deformation is not ornament; on this account it is what makes discourse discourse” converts exposition into endorsement. **Correction:** say “Todorov reports the Formalists as holding this; the passage develops their temporal-order argument through Vygotsky” unless a further passage explicitly demonstrates endorsement. Keep the quoted lineage claim, but remove the unproven adoption assertion.

2. **Greimas mediator generalisation — scope / calibration.** On p.30, the hero-asocial/mediator sentence is explicitly situated within the first isotopy and a preceding restriction to a sub-class of narratives; page 30 says “un grand nombre” rather than all narratives. The prose §4 ultimately notes the one-myth/method-procedure limit, but the tables at lines 83, 89 and checklist lines 149–151 turn the mediator into a reusable generic “protagonist” mechanism and ask whether reversal is person-caused as if this were a general design requirement. **Correction:** label these rows/checklist prompts “for Greimas's described narrative class / the Bororo procedure”; allow “no personal mediator” as an explicit non-fit, rather than treating that absence as an unfilled slot.

3. **Staged -23c/-24 folding claim — not present / unsupported.** The reviewed amendment does not mention -23c or -24 or claim their material has been folded elsewhere. It says the relevant sibling carries the five codes and level-count subject, and its sibling pointer names -23b. Thus the requested specific fold claim is neither contradicted nor verified by this file; do not infer it from the sibling pointer. **Correction:** if the frozen claim asserts folding elsewhere, identify the destination section/document and show the displaced material there; otherwise mark that assertion unresolved, not as this amendment's demonstrated result.
