### §3 Story and discourse — the split that decides what a world bible stores

The Chinese row draws the distinction that decides what a world bible holds: 故事 names the deep structure, the events in their causal order, and 话语 names the surface, the telling (S888, loc: PENDING).
Design consequence, and this file's reading: two records and not one. A culture can hold one 故事 in several 话语 — an oral telling, a written chronicle, a ritual recitation — and the differences between them are the part a worldbuilder can use.

| Question | Layer that answers it | What the world bible stores |
|---|---|---|
| what happened, in what causal order | 故事, the deep structure | the event chain, recorded without a teller |
| who tells it, in what order, at what speed, how often | 话语, the surface | one record per telling, with its choices |
| which telling is the authoritative one | neither layer | canon, which belongs to lore-shokunin |

### §4 The actantial model — roles that are not moral grades

The Chinese row presents the actantial model with Greimas's name attached, and the staged text adds the point that matters for a build: it is a vocabulary for a culture's roles that is not a moral one (S888, loc: PENDING).
The six slots are already in `myth-symbol/comparative-frameworks.md` §5 as the quick-notation for a quest-shaped narrative, so this file does not re-derive them; what it takes from the Chinese row is that the slots describe a role grammar rather than a ranking of characters by virtue (S888, loc: PENDING).
The table's questions are this file's reading; the slot names are the lane's comparative-frameworks §5.

| Slot | Question it asks of a character | Design note |
|---|---|---|
| subject | whose wanting moves the tale | the tale's engine is a desire, not an event |
| object | what is wanted | the object can be a person, a thing or a state |
| sender | who or what makes the wanting binding | the institution behind the quest, not the quest-giver's mood |
| receiver | who the outcome belongs to | separated from the subject, this is where a culture shows its idea of benefit |
| helper | who makes it possible | an alliance, and therefore a politics |
| opponent | who blocks it | an obstacle with a reason, never a moral grade |

Design consequence: a culture whose opponent slot is always the same social group has told you its boundary map, and a culture that leaves the receiver slot empty has told you something else. Neither reading is a moral verdict, which is the point of the distinction (this file's reading).

### §5 Time as three axes, not one

The Chinese row gives three time axes rather than the single axis of told order: 次序, 时长, 频率 (S888, loc: PENDING).
The glosses below are this file's, and they are pending against the opened page: order, duration and frequency — when an event is told against when it happens, how much telling an event is given, and how many times it is told.

| Axis | Question it answers | What changes when you set it |
|---|---|---|
| 次序 | is the telling in event order, or displaced | suspense and disclosure become design choices rather than accidents |
| 时长 | how much telling does an event get | pacing, and which events the culture treats as weighty |
| 频率 | how often is an event told | ritual repetition, variant tradition, official telling against folk telling |

Design consequence: three independent dials, so a world bible can record a culture's default settings on all three and mark the exceptions by genre (this file's reading).

### §6 起承転結 as a completable unit

The Japanese half records 起承転結 as a completable minimal unit rather than as a four-beat plot (S889, loc: PENDING; if the scout report places the term in the other Japanese row, this line moves with its id).
Design consequence: the term is a claim about when a short form is finished, not a beat sheet to be stretched over a novel-scale plot.

| Use | What it gives | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| as a four-beat template | an easy outline for any length | it flattens long plots and hides where a culture's own endings fall |
| as a completable unit | a test of whether a short form is done | it says nothing about length, and cannot pace a long work |

### §8 A three-layer description format

The first Japanese row records a three-layer machine-writable description format — ストーリー, クエスト, シーン — with キャラクター as a hierarchical sub-layer (S889, loc: PENDING).
The ordering and the sub-layer's attachment below are the staged text's, and they are pending against the opened page; the design questions are this file's reading.

| Layer | Position as the staged text names it | Design question it forces |
|---|---|---|
| ストーリー | the whole | what is this work, in one record a machine can read |
| クエスト | a delimited arc inside the whole | where does this arc begin and close, and who drives it |
| シーン | the unit inside the arc | what changes in this unit, and who is present |
| キャラクター | a hierarchical sub-layer, not a fourth level | which record does a character hang from, and what happens when it hangs from two |

The second Japanese row carries a hierarchical structural description of narrative as its §2 (S890, loc: PENDING). The correspondence between that description and the three-layer format is this file's reading, not the row's claim, and it is the reason the two rows are read together here.
Design consequence: a world bible meant to be machine-writable stores whole, arc and unit at three levels, and a character is a view across them rather than a store of its own (this file's reading).

### §9 The generation side

The second Japanese row also carries the Japanese narratology of narrative generation, 物語生成の物語論, which models how stories are made and not only how they read (S890, loc: PENDING).

| Side | The question it answers | What a worldbuilder does with it |
|---|---|---|
| reading side | what does this telling do to its audience | audits a finished tale |
| making side | by what procedure does this culture produce a tale | records the procedure as an institution, with its stages and its gatekeepers |

Design consequence: the making side turns a poetics into an institution, which is the form a world bible can actually store (this file's reading).
