## 2. Attacks on the likely entry wording

1. **Counterexample to a blunt R on Afonso (false provenance risk).** The primary letter's strongest sentence supports the popular claim: "we do not wish to carry out trade and export slaves" (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-1). Even a scholarly endorsement on the publisher's page writes "his efforts in fighting the Portuguese slave trade" (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-3). A bank row reading just "R: Afonso never opposed the slave trade" would be contradicted by the letter. Impacted axes: calibration, sourced claim. Smallest safe correction: say R for "abolitionist in principle", keep the letter's request bounded to July 1526, quote its grievance (kidnapped freeborn subjects) and pair it with Heywood's abstract. The inspection board of October 1526 stays T: I did not open a primary for it (see unopened).
2. **"Syncretism" must not be coded R.** Thornton, the source of the correction, uses the word himself ("much more tolerant of syncretism in Kongo"). The contested part is "thin veneer / superficial". Impacted axis: calibration. Correction: C on "superficial", with Thornton (1984) as one side and the older opinion he names as the other; no settled verdict.
3. **Cosmogram identity slippage.** "Cosmogram" is a 1970 English coinage, the diagram's attestations in the opened source are 19th-century Cuba and Fu-Kiau's 20th-century writing, and MacGaffey's note 56 calls a saying Fu-Kiau uses "a mistaken version" of a nganga's song. An entry that writes "the Kongo cosmogram (dikenga)" as a kingdom-era doctrine claims an age no opened row carries. Impacted axes: calibration, coverage. Correction: name the coinage and date, mark the reading C, any 15th to 17th-century claim T, and point one-way to `myth-symbol/cosmology-cosmography.md` (which owns cosmograms generally and has no Kongo content at base) without restating it.
4. **Era and identity slippage on minkisi.** The Met's Mangaaka figure is Yombe-Kongo work, ca. 1880 to 1900, from the Chiloango River region (the record's Geography field: Republic of the Congo or Cabinda, Angola, Chiloango River; Culture field: Kongo) (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-9). It is evidence for late-19th-century coastal minkisi, not for the court of Mbanza Kongo/São Salvador in the 16th century. MacGaffey's abstract calls "Kongo" "an ill-defined entity (a tribe, a kingdom, a culture, a region?)". Impacted axes: coverage, calibration. Correction: the entry declares on first use whether "Kongo" means the kingdom, the Kikongo-speaking peoples, or the region, and dates every object row.
5. **"Empire" is used by scholars too.** Hendrickx (2021) writes "the Empire of Kongo" (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-14). Marking the word R would overreach. Correction: R only for "ruling Central Africa"; H for the label; B for UNESCO's "one of the largest constituted states in Southern Africa", carrying the provinces and the Mbata exception from Lopes.
6. **Kikongo singular in the sources themselves.** UNESCO writes "the Kikongo language" (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-5). The entry may use the singular for a named historical variety (for example the 1624 catechism's) but must say which; the cluster classification should not be presented as settled beyond what Glottolog catalogues. The Persée text of de Schryver et al. (2015) was masked; see unopened.
7. **Kimpa Vita analogy as reception, not fact.** CDAMM attributes the label to Kaké (1976); Hendrickx attributes it to Vansina. Both are reception. One open web page (Bitter Winter, unopened beyond search) claims an army of 20,000; no opened row carries it, so it is T at most. Correction: H, labelled as a later commemorative frame a worldbuilder may give to an in-world speaker.
8. **"No writing" correction must keep its own limit.** The record is real but skewed: for the Antonian crisis only one letter from a Kongolese author survives, and the movement is known "mostly through Italian priests" (NEW-CriticCalibrationKongo-12, p. 3). Correction: R for "no history", B note on missionary mediation, in line with the lane's colonial-mediation rule (`civilizations/igbo.md:189`, `civilizations/mali-songhai.md:198`).
