numeral notations universally progress toward a perfect decimal positional
system is rejected for the entry, and the opened source says so in its own
words. Source: https://journals.openedition.org/terrain/17506 — accessed
2026-11-04 — §"Classifying numerical notations", paragraph 10 (2018): 「This
avoids the teleological argument of Ifrah (1987) and Dehaene (1997) that sees
decimal, positional numeration as the end-point of a developmental sequence
towards perfection.」 and 「In fact, using this typology it is evident that
there is no perfect system.」 Grounds: the source quote supports no-perfect-
system, so the teleological universal claim is refused; the entry must present
the typology as a classification with no summit. Flip: admit the universal
progression claim only if a source is opened that states it as its own finding
and the no-perfect-system sentence is withdrawn. Re-test tool and verbatim
output: receipts/reject-retest.txt. This candidate is a Gate rejection of a
CLAIM, not a registry row; it consumes no S-id and no used-by cell.
