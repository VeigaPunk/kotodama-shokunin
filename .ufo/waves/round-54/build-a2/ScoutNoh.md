- Decide what the protection covers: Japan designated Nôgaku an Important Intangible Cultural Property in 1957, and the National Noh Theatre (founded 1983) runs courses to train actors in the leading roles — the institution protects the practitioners and the training, not the objects alone (NEW-ScoutNoh-1, description, third paragraph).

- Write the animation technique into training: since the mask does not move, the wearer controls its expression by tilting the head to work light and shadow (NEW-ScoutNoh-2, Description, sentence 5).
