### Rule

A repair must change the producer or its explicit contract, demonstrate that the old failure is detectable, preserve an unaffected control, and bind its new output to the exact measured inputs. A passing regeneration, unchanged count, filed disclosure, or digest agreement cannot by itself establish semantic preservation or family isolation. A documented disposition can resolve a historical discrepancy without claiming the original bytes now pass; it cannot substitute for a source fix where the declared claim requires one.

Keep **historical verdict**, **current reproduction**, and **later supersession** as distinct fields. A newer green does not erase an older rejection; a newer red does not automatically invalidate an older measurement. Explain which revision, selected artifact, environment, and invariant changed before assigning a cause. Retain command, date, input/runner digests or revisions, expected result, observed result, owner, and evidence pointer for each disposition.
