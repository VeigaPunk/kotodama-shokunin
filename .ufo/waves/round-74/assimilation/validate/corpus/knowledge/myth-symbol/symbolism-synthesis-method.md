## 1. What a symbolic grammar is — a generator, not an encyclopedia

- A symbolic grammar is the total system by which a culture maps a small closed inventory of signs — the five canonical registers: **color, animal, number, celestial body, direction** — onto a small closed inventory of value-domains (sovereignty, purity, death, fertility, deceit, liminality...), **such that the mapping between any two registers is itself fixed and learnable**. An encyclopedia of associations ("owl = wisdom") is not a grammar; a grammar answers "what would this culture make of a white cricket?" without a designer vote.
- A grammar is specified by five objects: (1) the **registers**; (2) the **base partition** — how many slots each register splits into and from what real enumeration; (3) the **alignment matrix** — which slot of register A maps to which slot of register B; (4) each symbol's **primary domain** plus its **licensed satellites**; (5) the **era table** — which meaning holds when.
- Real closed systems to imitate structurally, not cosmetically:
  - **Wuxing** (five phases): one partition (wood/fire/earth/metal/water) run through colors, directions, seasons, creatures, organs, tastes — an administrative grid the state applied to robes, city plans and medicine (Needham 1956; Zhouli "Kaogong ji": east–qing/azure dragon, south–chi/vermilion bird, west–bai/white tiger, north–hei/black tortoise-snake, center–huang/yellow).
  - **The Yucatec Maya quincunx**: red = east, white = north, black = west, yellow = south, yax green-blue = center (Sharer & Traxler 2006); the Mexica encode the same four-plus-center logic as a cosmogram with a world-tree in each corner (Codex Fejérváry-Mayer p. 1; Boone 2000).
  - **The Ndembu color triad**: white/red/black, each spreading through the whole ritual vocabulary — white mudyi milk = motherhood, matriliny, goodness; red = blood, hunting, womanhood; black = sorcery, badness (Turner 1966).
  - **The medieval Latin package**: bestiary + lapidary + numerology + heraldic code — a closed code that is manufacturable, teachable, and checkable (White 1954; Hopper 1938; Fox-Davies 1909).
- The lane's core design constraint: real systems are **multivocal** — Turner's dominant symbols condense sensory, ideological, and normative meaning at once. A world bible cannot absorb uncontrolled multivocality; it reads as contradiction at the first cross-reference. Hence controlled multivocality (§5): satellites are allowed, but each must be derived by a named transformation.
- Standing rule (mirrors comparative-frameworks.md §1): the grammar is **emic** for the fictional culture; this procedure is **etic** designer tooling — never ascribe it. In-world scholars express the grammar as numerology, list-exegesis, riddle tradition, and antiquarian squabbling, never as an alignment matrix (couples comparative-frameworks §7).

## 2. The five frameworks the method is built on

| Framework | What it contributes to the grammar | Calibration |
|---|---|---|
| Turner — dominant symbols, multivocality (1966, 1967) | a symbol has a sensory pole and an ideological pole; design both | exegesis is Ndembu-specific; a worked method, not a typology |
| Lévi-Strauss — systems of transformations (1962) | a sign's value is its position in the system; registers transform into each other; totems are "good to think" | "totemism" is largely an analyst's fiction (Totemism 1962) — build the classificatory logic, never "a totemic culture" |
| Leach — anomalous animals, symbolic abuse (1964, 1976) | the category-crossing animal (bat: bird/mammal) attracts taboo and insult; cultures run codes with traffic rules | an English-language data set — the anomaly logic travels, the inventory does not |
| Douglas — anomaly becomes pollution (1966) | where the grammar is violated the culture generates ritual response (the Leviticus pig fails the hoof/cud conjunction) | take the anomaly engine only; her grid/group sociology is a separate tool |
| Peirce/Eco/Saussure — sign types and arbitrariness | separates icon (resemblance), index (material cause), symbol (convention); forces "why THIS sign?" | Sperber (1975): symbolism may be ordinary cognition plus relevance — the matrix is a design tool, not a claim about minds |

- The Peirce split is the workhorse: **icons** motivate from resemblance, **indexes** from material connection (dye cost, bird migration, heliacal rising), **symbols** from convention alone. A grammar with zero indexes is fragile decoration (see §5 R4).

## 3. The synthesis procedure (steps 0–8)

0. **Fix the material substrate**: subsistence, ecology, available dyestuffs, calendar, base numeral, media (what can be woven, carved, minted, printed). Everything below must be derivable from this (couples culture-material).
1. **Choose 3–5 load-bearing polarity axes** (hot/cold, wet/dry, pure/impure, raw/cooked, high/low). These are the axes that inversions and satellites will run on (Douglas 1966; comparative-frameworks §4).
2. **Choose the base partition n from a real enumeration of the culture**, not from aesthetics: cardinal directions plus center (5), seasons or tides, moons per year, days per the week, councils or provinces (the "Yugong" nine provinces), kin-classes. n must recur in the culture's actual counting life (Schimmel 1993; §4.3).
3. **Fill each register over the same n slots with motivated picks**: colors from available dyes, animals from local fauna with their behavior, celestial bodies from the sky the culture actually watches, directions from the culture's frame of reference (§4.5).
4. **Write the alignment matrix**; where a register refuses to align (seven planets over five slots), mark the cell **UNALIGNED** with a reason — never force smoothness. Real systems have lumps, and lumps are where in-world controversy lives.
5. **Assign primary domains and satellites**: exactly one primary domain per symbol; satellites only via the transformation set (§5 R1, R3).
6. **Version it**: record the grammar's state per era (§6) — the initial state is just row one.
7. **Run the audit** (§7). Fix collisions by splitting signs or demoting satellites; minute every fix.
8. **Hand off**: package per §10 for lore-shokunin.

## 4. Register construction notes (real exemplars per register)

### 4.1 Color

- Lexicon rules (how many basic terms, which stage) live in linguistics/color-terminology.md — keep this register consistent with the language files. The grammar layer adds:
- **Status grammar is dye grammar**: scarce, stable dyes key elite colors — shellfish purple's monopoly; blue's rise from a marginal hue to the Capetian royal color across the 12th–13th c. as dye practice and devotional politics shifted (Pastoureau 2001). A fictional court color must answer "where does the dye come from, who can afford it, what law gates it?" (couples culture-material, costume).
- **Falsified universal — keep it falsified**: white = bridal purity in Europe vs white = mourning across much of East and South Asia; red = bridal luck in China vs red = danger in industrial signage. Domains are cultural assignments, not physics. No handoff sentence may say "white means purity" unqualified.
- **Graphic constraint**: a closed code imposes well-formedness on its own rendering — heraldry's **rule of tincture** (metal on colour; Fox-Davies 1909). Every fictional grammar should invent its analog: a compositional rule that makes malformed combinations visibly wrong — a blasphemy you can see.

### 4.2 Animal

- **The anomaly picks your taboo** (Leach 1964; Douglas 1966): the animal that crosses a category boundary carries the pollution load. Real exemplars: the bat in insult vocabulary (bird/quadruped); the pig failing the Leviticus conjunction; the **barnacle goose** — grown from driftwood, therefore "fish", therefore eaten in Lent (Gerald of Wales, Topographia Hibernica, c. 1188; White 1954). Assign one boundary-straddler per major boundary in the culture and load it.
- **Prey apologetics**: a hunting culture that keys a prey animal symbolically generates apology and release rites for the kill — the Circumpolar bear ceremonial is the classic case (Hallowell 1926). An animal taboo implies a rite; write both or neither.
- **Rebus and naming load**: homophones make symbols cheap — Chinese bat 蝠 fú ≈ 福 fú "fortune", deer 鹿 lù ≈ 禄 lù "emolument" (Bartholomew 2006). If the conlang has homophones (couples linguistics files), the grammar farms them: this is where the language and symbol registers actually couple.
- **Distribution as treaty layer**: real emblematic fauna (lion, eagle) are spread by political geography, not virtue; two neighbors flying the same lion is an inheritance claim or a forgery. Track emblem distribution like borders.

### 4.3 Number

- **The enumeration rule**: a number symbol must trace to a real count in the culture — calendar units, administrative divisions, kin-classes, market units. 12 is strong because zodiac → months → tribes → apostles chain off one schematic year; 9 in China is anchored to the "Yugong" nine provinces; 40 is the Biblical "many" idiom; 3 rides Greco-Roman triadic habit (Schimmel 1993; Hopper 1938).
- **Homophone numbers are East Asian normal**: 四 sì "four" ≈ 死 sǐ "death" (tetraphobia in floors and hospital rooms); Cantonese 8 發 faat "prosper" (Bartholomew 2006). A conlang with near-homophony predicts its own lucky and cursed numbers — derive them, don't decorate.
- **Practice counts the number or it is not load-bearing**: 108 beads on a mala against a traditional count of afflictions is the model (Schimmel 1993).
- Calibration: in-world numerology is emic practice and should be rich; designer-side "hidden code" claims about the grammar are etic fraud (§8).

### 4.4 Celestial

- **One body, two identities must be resolved**: Inanna/Ištar carries both morning-star and evening-star aspects. Either split the body into two signs with an era-table note, or own the ambiguity as in-world doctrine — never leave it as silent contradiction.
- **Calendar bodies beat zodiac bodies**: the load-bearing sky is the sky that tells time — Pleiades heliacal risings as planting markers across many traditions; Sirius at the Egyptian New Year (Aveni 2001, 1989). Pick celestial symbols by what the culture watches, not by Western constellation furniture.
- **Gender and polarity are free variables**: Amaterasu is a sun-goddess; Sól is a sun-goddess; Helios and Surya are male. Never import the sun-masculine/moon-feminine default; assign from the culture's own polarity axes.
- **Alignment claims are checkable** (Ruggles 2015): "the temple faces midwinter sunrise" must survive a planetarium run at a stated date and precision. Handoffs carry the tolerance figure.

### 4.5 Direction

- **Frame of reference first** (Levinson 2003; Brown 1983): cardinal terms typically derive from sun-path, winds, or river flow; many cultures run landmark systems instead — mauka/makai (seaward/landward) in Hawaiʻi, uphill/downhill in Tzeltal. A compass culture can abstract N/E/S/W; a landmark culture's "north" is not a concept. Declare the frame before assigning any colors or animals to directions.
- **The 4+1 quincunx is the common closed partition**: four directions plus center, each with color, creature, season (§1 exemplars). It is popular with builders because it is checkable — a five-slot matrix that must cohere across five registers at once.
- **Anti-universalist default**: direction-color pairs are conventional (Maya east = red; Chinese east = qing green-blue). Two fictional cultures never share a direction-color pair without a documented diffusion link (couples language-contact).

## 5. Consistency rules

- **R1 — One symbol, one primary domain.** Each symbol gets exactly one primary domain: the default reading wherever the symbol appears. This is a lane rule for bible coherence, not a claim about real cultures (real cultures exceed it constantly — that is Turner's multivocality). Satellites capped at 3, each citing a transformation (R3).
- **R2 — Closure over the base partition.** Every register fills the same n slots, or marks UNALIGNED cells with reasons. The alignment matrix is the artifact lore-shokunin can diff.
- **R3 — Licensed transformations only.** Satellite meanings must derive from one of: metonymy/synecdoche; metaphor along a declared axis; inversion on a named polarity; event-index; borrowing (donor + era); homophone rebus; taboo-shift. Undocumented satellite meanings are audit failures.
- **R4 — Grounding quota.** Every symbol carries a stated sensory or material ground (behavior, chemistry, ecology, astronomy). At least a third of the inventory must be indexical, not stipulated (§2) — that is what makes the grammar generative instead of decorative.
- **R5 — No unmarked synonymy.** Two symbols sharing a primary domain (two "purity" whites) require a stated contrast axis (material: chalk vs linen; register: court vs kitchen).
- **R6 — Inversion needs its axis named.** A white/black or raw/cooked reversal must declare which polarity axis it runs on; unstated inversions read as contradiction.
- **R7 — Productivity test.** The grammar must predict readings for novel compounds (the kenning standard). Three fresh compounds per audit round, minuted.
- **R8 — Emic/etic tags on every artifact.** The matrix is designer tooling; in-world exegesis is numerology and riddle tradition (couples linguistic-anthropology; comparative-frameworks §7).

## 6. Change rules — symbols drift with eras

| Mechanism | What moves | Real exemplar |
|---|---|---|
| M1 material/economic | dye, trade, technology re-rank a color or emblem | blue: marginal → royal in Capetian France (Pastoureau 2001); synthetic mauve collapses scarcity meaning (Garfield 2000) |
| M2 political/religious overwrite | conquest or revolution re-keys the dynasty's symbols | French Revolution: Bourbon white vs the tricolore |
| M3 media standardization | print, coinage, signage freeze or spread an iconography | planetary week and zodiac diffusion across the Roman world (Aveni 1989) |
| M4 linguistic | semantic change re-keys the sign's word | pejoration (OE sælig "blessed" → silly); taboo cycling, euphemism drift (Traugott & Dasher 2002; couples politeness-taboo-euphemism) |
| M5 contact/borrowing | wholesale import of a donor system, donor visible | Buddhist lotus motifs along the trade roads; rebus symbolism riding Chinese writing (Bartholomew 2006) |
| M6 event-index | a datable event anchors an omen or emblem | Picasso's 1949 La Colombe lithograph fixing the dove as the peace-movement emblem |

- **Drift law 1 — date-stamp every meaning.** The era table is the artifact: {symbol, meaning, valid-from, valid-to?, mechanism M1–M6}.
- **Drift law 2 — no retro-projection (the anachronism check).** Meanings do not flow backward: pink = girls is a mid-20th-c. North American retail consolidation, and early-20th-c. US advice literature often prescribed pink for boys (Paoletti 2012). Any bible sentence that lets a present meaning explain the past fails audit.
- **Drift law 3 — closure survives change.** When one slot re-keys (say, red becomes the smiths' color), the aligned slots — its direction, animal, number — are re-audited the same round.
- **Drift law 4 — leave fossils on purpose.** Dead meanings survive in fixed collocations, heraldry, idioms — Japan's green traffic light still named ao shingō is the type-case (color-terminology.md §4). Fossils are era markers and in-world antiquarian squabbles; budget at least one per era.

## 7. The symbol-audit checklist

Run over any draft before handoff; log each line's result.

Inventory:
1. Register tables exist (color, animal, number, celestial, direction) with columns: symbol | primary domain | ground | era.
2. One primary domain per symbol; satellites ≤3, each citing a transformation (R1, R3).
3. Alignment matrix filled; UNALIGNED cells carry reasons (R2).
Coherence:
4. No two symbols share a primary domain without a stated contrast axis (R5).
5. Every inversion names its polarity axis (R6).
6. Cross-register polarity coherence: each loaded axis maps consistently; exceptions era-stamped.
7. Productivity: three novel compounds predicted and minuted (R7).
Grounding:
8. Every symbol has a stated sensory/material ground; ≥1/3 of the inventory indexical (R4).
9. Colors answerable to dyestuffs and sumptuary law; numbers anchored to real counts; celestial claims planetarium-checked with tolerance; direction frame of reference declared (§4).
10. No unqualified universal claim ("white = purity") anywhere in the draft.
Drift:
11. Era table present; every meaning date-stamped with a mechanism from M1–M6.
12. No retro-projection — the anachronism check passes (drift law 2).
13. Post-change neighbor audit logged for every re-keyed slot (drift law 3).
14. Borrowed symbols marked with donor culture and adoption era; fossils identified per era (drift law 4).
Handoff:
15. Emic/etic tags applied (R8); no framework vocabulary in emic mouths.
16. Collision log complete: every fix minuted (split signs, demoted satellites, declared UNALIGNED).
17. Language coupling checked: homophone and naming load verified against the conlang's actual lexicon (§4.2–4.3; couples linguistics).
18. Package complete per §10; nothing in it decides canon.

## 8. Calibration — contested and discredited, plainly

- **Jungian archetype dictionaries and pop dream-symbol tables** (Jung ed. 1964 and descendants): no cross-cultural evidence for fixed archetypal content; usable only as a vocabulary shelf. The Jung/Campbell calibration in comparative-frameworks.md §2 applies here with full force.
- **Eliade-style universal symbolisms** (sun = father, water = dissolution): design language, not scholarship — comparative-frameworks.md §3 covers it; the standing rule "generate with the framework, never ascribe it" is this file's R8.
- **Pop color psychology**: single-study headlines ("red wins contests" — Hill & Barton 2005, Nature 435) have contested follow-ups and never license a culture-wide "red = aggression" domain assignment. If a fictional ideology asserts such links in-world, that is excellent emic material; as a designer claim it is noise.
- **Totemism as a culture type**: Lévi-Strauss himself showed the category is largely an analyst's artifact (Totemism 1962). Build classificatory systems, not "totemic peoples".
- **Bible-code numerology**: Witztum, Rips & Rosenberg (1994, Statistical Science 9) claimed equidistant-letter codes in Genesis; McKay, Bar-Natan, Bar-Hillel & Kalai (1999, Statistical Science 14) refuted it as a tuning artifact. Lane use: in-world numerologists welcome; designer-side "hidden code" claims about your own grammar are the same error.
- **Berlin–Kay stages as a timeline**: superseded; use the stage list only as a lexicon-consistency checklist (color-terminology.md §2–3).
- **Sperber's caution (1975) stands over the whole apparatus**: the alignment matrix is designer scaffolding for coherence, not a model of how symbol systems arise in minds. Real systems are lumpier than any matrix you will hand off — preserve the lumps (§3 step 4).

## 9. Worked micro-example — one estuarine culture through the procedure

- Substrate (step 0): tidal estuary farmers; dyes = tannin-and-iron black, shell-lime white, madder-root red, kelp olive; base numeral 5 = the four tide-directions plus the slack ("the quiet fifth").
- Partition and axes (steps 1–2): 4+1; wet/dry, flood/ebb, pure/impure.
- Registers (steps 3–4): directions by tide-flow and shore-light, not sun — floodward, ebbward, dawnshore, duskshore, center. Animals: heron (floodward), crab (sideways-walker — the anomaly, hence the judge's beast), gull (ebbward), eel (duskshore), oyster (center: makes its own pearl). Numbers 5 and 25. Celestial: Pleiades heliacal rising opens planting — one checkable claim, tolerance ±2 days, Ruggles-method.
- Matrix (step 4): eel refuses a color (skin reads differently wet vs dry) — cell marked UNALIGNED, recorded as in-world doctrine "the eel has no color", hence the oath-beast for words under test. The lump became content: the desired failure mode.
- Primary domains (step 5): white = salt/purity (ground: the shell-lime ritual crust); mourning-white logged as a metonymic satellite (salt strewed on the dead), not a primary.
- Drift (step 6): in the iron-trade era, red re-keys from blood/flood-plain to smithcraft (M1 + M5); neighbor audit re-keys ebbward from rust-red to imported grey; the fossil idiom "red ebb" survives as an era marker (drift law 4).
- Audit (step 7): catches two purity symbols (white salt vs oyster-pearl) → contrast axis declared (surface crust vs hidden core; R5). Productivity compounds minuted: "grey gull oath" = a pledge collateral on the ebb tide's return.
- Handoff (step 8): matrix + era table + open to-verify list (the Pleiades date must be re-run once the world's final latitude is set).

## 10. Handoff format and canon boundary

- Package per culture for lore-shokunin: register tables, alignment matrix, era table, collision log, samples of emic exegesis (how in-world scholars state the grammar), and the open to-verify list. Reference and method only; which reading is true in-world, which dynasty owns which color — canon, owned by lore-shokunin (AGENTS.md).
- Lane-name reminder: the kotodama doctrine — Man'yōshū 894 (Yamanoue no Okura) praises Yamato as "the land blessed by the word-spirit" (kotodama no saiwafu kuni) — is the standing example that a lexicon itself carries symbol load: speech acts, names, and formulae sit inside the grammar this file builds (couples orality-formulaic-language, naming-systems). Words are the sixth register; treat them as such whenever a culture claims them efficacious.
