# Verification Method Overview — Testable Coupling, Separated Evidence, Fail-Closed Handoffs

- topic: the lane's craft-verification philosophy: every language–myth–symbol–era–costume system claim must identify a corpus decision rule, inspectable inputs, an expected result, and a possible failure; observations, inferences, unknowns, and proposed design choices remain separate; missing evidence never becomes permission to assert canon.
- sources:
  - Stephen E. Toulmin (1958; updated ed. 2003), *The Uses of Argument*, Cambridge University Press, especially “The Layout of Arguments”; [publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/uses-of-argument/26CF801BC12004587B66778297D5567C). Argument structure, not a historical-truth detector.
  - Alison Wylie (2002), *Thinking from Things: Essays in the Philosophy of Archaeology*, University of California Press, especially chapters 9, 11, and 16 on analogy, evidential cables, and convergence; [publisher contents](https://www.ucpress.edu/books/thinking-from-things/paper). Archaeological reasoning, not a license to equate artifacts with beliefs.
  - Michel-Rolph Trouillot (1995), *Silencing the Past: Power and the Production of History*, Beacon Press. Power enters the production of historical evidence and narratives; archival abundance is not neutral representation.
  - UNESCO, Intergovernmental Committee for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage (2015), *Ethical Principles for Safeguarding Intangible Cultural Heritage*, Decision 10.COM 15.a, especially principles 4, 5, and 8; [official text](https://ich.unesco.org/en/ethics-and-ich-00866). An ethical safeguarding benchmark, not a legal permission ruling for fiction.
  - Internal method authorities: `SKILL.md` decision table and checklist; the seeded knowledge files routed in §5. Their design constraints and historical assertions require different tests.
- couples: **language** — historical-linguistics, naming-systems, writing-systems; **myth** — comparative-frameworks and named narrative variants; **symbol** — symbolism-synthesis-method and color-symbolism; **era** — era-periodization-models and language stages; **costume/material** — textile-technology, costume-semiotics, material-culture-inference-method; **siblings** — lore-shokunin receives findings and candidate systems, chousa-shokunin owns general source-research method; no sibling or world-bible writes.
- verified-at: 2026-10-17 — round-8 metadata pass: header fields and cross-reference resolution checked (couples targets are named at entry level and resolve); 3 linked locators re-checked for resolution (2 reachable, 1 blocked by remote server); argument structures not re-inspected.
- recheck: when the corpus decision rules it cites are revised, or before a PASS is carried into a handoff as though it were canon.

## 1. Contract: verify the claim at its actual strength

- This is a **manual craft-verification specification**, not an implemented script or evidence that an automated gate has run. A future executable gate needs its own registered command, fixtures, and verdict contract.
- Lane policy: every system claim must be testable against the corpus's own decision tables, numbered rules, or application checklists; “feels authentic” is not a result.
- **Internal coherence** asks whether a proposed system follows its declared rules. **Reference adequacy** asks whether the real-world evidence supports the analogy or assertion. Passing either does not imply passing the other.
- **Canon authority is a separate question:** a coherent, well-referenced proposal still belongs to lore-shokunin for acceptance, rejection, or revision. PASS here means admissible at the stated scope, never true in-world by decree.
- Testability is not compulsory quantification: a qualitative inference can name its warrant, competing reading, and defeating evidence. An aesthetic preference must instead be declared as a design choice with observable implementation constraints.
- Corpus membership is not proof. A confident seed sentence, a large bibliography, or a previously green checklist cannot repair an unsupported causal bridge.
- The rules and verdict vocabulary below are **lane policy**, informed by the cited methods; they are not a published universal standard for reconstructing cultures.

## 2. Keep epistemic type separate from viewpoint and authority

| Tag | Meaning | Minimum record | What it never licenses |
|---|---|---|---|
| O — observation | a bounded trace or reported observation | object/text identifier, edition or catalog locator, observer, date/context, what was actually inspected | treating “the edict prohibits X” as “nobody wore X” |
| I — inference | a conclusion connected to observations by an explicit warrant | parent O/I IDs, reasoning, relevant comparison, alternatives, possible defeater | treating interpretation as a visible property of an artifact |
| U — unknown | unresolved, inaccessible, contradictory, untranslated, or untested information | exact missing datum, bounded search or inspection, consequence, next check `(to-verify)` | silently filling the gap with a familiar tradition |
| D — design stipulation | an explicitly invented candidate rule or assumption | chosen value, scope, alternatives, dependencies, reproducible consequences | claiming real-world attestation or approval by lore |

- Split compound sentences: “the robe is red and therefore the wearer is a priest” needs an O about the trace and an I about identity, not one O covering both.
- If using a catalog rather than the object, record **“the catalog reports fiber X”**; do not imply personal examination or independent laboratory confirmation.
- Add a separate **viewpoint** field: participant/emic, analyst/etic, or designer. An emic statement can be observed as a statement while its cosmological content remains unverified.
- Add a separate **authority** field: source author, in-world claimant, proposing designer, or canon owner. Repetition by a powerful institution establishes neither compliance nor metaphysical truth.
- Confidence is scoped to a proposition, not inherited from a source's prestige. Use justified labels such as well-supported, conditional, or thin; do not manufacture numerical probabilities.
- U is not false, I is not automatically weak, and D is not inferior craft. The failure is presenting one as another.

## 3. Minimal claim card: nothing untraceable enters a verdict

| Field | Required contents |
|---|---|
| Claim ID and proposition | one falsifiable or explicitly stipulated assertion; split conjunctions with different evidence |
| Scope | culture/group, region, speaker/wearer/viewer, genre or occasion, era interval, exclusions |
| Type and viewpoint | O/I/U/D plus emic/etic/designer; identify whose knowledge is represented |
| Provenance | author + year + edition; page, passage, object/catalog ID, or stable section; original language and translator where relevant |
| Dependencies | parent claim IDs; distinguish independent evidence from retellings of the same source |
| Rule locator | corpus filename + heading/table + matching row or numbered rule; preserve the reviewed file version or digest |
| Test and expected result | concrete input, predicate, expected output or permitted range, and a failing case fixed before review |
| Actual result | inspected output, mismatch, or missing input; reviewer identity and date; no result supplied from memory |
| Limits and disposition | alternatives, calibration status, open `(to-verify)` items, verdict, and exact downstream blocks |

- For a generation claim, supply a fresh input: a new name, symbolic compound, garment context, or era-boundary case. Reproducing the author's worked example alone tests copying, not productivity.
- For an empirical reference claim, the corpus rule supplies the **question**; a checkable external trace supplies the **evidence**. Generated fictional examples cannot corroborate real history.
- If a locator is not checked, label that locator `(to-verify)` rather than inventing a page number. A publisher description verifies a book's identity, not every claim attributed to it.

## 4. Named frameworks translated into bounded craft checks

| Framework | Use here | Calibration boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Toulmin's argument layout (1958/2003) | separate grounds, claim, warrant, backing, qualifier, and rebuttal; expose the missing bridge from a robe to a rank | a complete argument can still have false premises |
| Wylie's analogy and evidential convergence (2002) | state relevant similarities, disanalogies, and whether different evidence lines constrain the same interpretation | two websites copying one chronicle are one evidential lineage, not independent convergence |
| Trouillot's historical silences (1995) | ask who produced, preserved, selected, translated, and made the record authoritative | neither a court archive nor its absence transparently represents all social groups |
| UNESCO ethical principles (2015) | respect living communities, changing practice, consent, and customary access when using reference material | public availability is not a blanket invitation to reproduce restricted knowledge; rights decisions remain out of province |

## 5. Route each claim to the corpus's actual decision surface

Paths in this table are relative to `corpus/knowledge/`; the headings are locators, not assertions that every sentence in those files is established scholarship.

| Claim family | Corpus surface | Evidence/test input | Failure or hold condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| inheritance, subgroup, daughter form | `linguistics/historical-linguistics.md` §2 correspondence decision table, §6 dating, §7 law-list procedure | correspondence sets; ordered laws; loan/analogy strata; fresh derivation | lookalike asserted as descent; unexplained output; unattested date presented as exact |
| person/place/divine name | `linguistics/naming-systems.md` §7 derivation pipeline | phonology, morphology, name slots, stratum, address/taboo context | unlicensed form or slot order; folk etymology presented as reconstruction |
| script–language fit | `linguistics/writing-systems.md` §6 profile table and §7 pitfalls | phonotactics, sign inventory, reading conventions, medium, sample inscription | missing encoding strategy; script shape used to infer language family; best-fit heuristic treated as necessity |
| myth interpretation or generator | `myth-symbol/comparative-frameworks.md` §6 framework table and §7 calibration | identified variant, genre, narrator/context, framework, rival reading | universal monomyth asserted as evidence; generator mistaken for a community's self-description |
| symbolic reading or new compound | `myth-symbol/symbolism-synthesis-method.md` §5 R1–R8 and §7 audit | scoped matrix, primary/satellite readings, transformation, fresh compound | unexplained collision, missing transformation, era-free universal color meaning |
| period or transition | `culture-material/era-periodization-models.md` §2 typology, §4 seam logistics, §12 calibration | calendar/reckoning authority, boundary rule, conversion assumptions, region | cosmic age taken as measured date; universal metal-age ladder; undefined transition day |
| makeable garment or production term | `culture-material/textile-technology.md` §1 four gates and §6 vocabulary table | fiber, dye, loom, finishing; imports and knowledge channels; proposed term/referent | production claimed without a viable route; availability, knowledge, and local manufacture conflated |
| costume signal or enforcement | `culture-material/costume-semiotics.md` §3 channel grid, §4 law/practice split, §9 protocol | term, carrier, wearer, viewer, context, sanction/enforcement, era | law treated as universal behavior; viewer omitted; written costume mistaken for worn costume |
| artifact-to-institution/belief | `culture-material/material-culture-inference-method.md` §2 ladder and §5 failure modes | trace, formation/survival context, inference bridge, relevant analogy | “pots = people”; unexplained deposit labeled ritual; tier alone used as proof |

- A procedural file can supply a decision surface even without a literal table; record the exact step and a falsifiable predicate. “Consult linguistics” is not a locator.
- **Policy versus scholarship:** the symbol file's primary-domain constraint and grounding quota are designer constraints, not measured universals. Test declared adoption and compliance, not whether real societies obey them.
- Likewise, a textile production constraint does not prove that speakers cannot name an imported, remembered, described, or imagined cloth. Require the relevant access/knowledge channel; local inability to manufacture is not lexical impossibility.
- HARD/SOFT/DOCTRINE/DESIGN labels in the material-inference file are received metadata, not automatic permissions. Re-express each proposition as O/I/U/D and preserve its original label without granting canon status.
- A table conflict or unsupported absolute is a finding, not an invitation to choose whichever file permits a green result. Hold the affected claim; report the conflicting locators to the corpus owner.
- If no applicable rule exists, record a coverage gap and a candidate test. Do not claim that an unreviewed ad hoc test is an established corpus gate.

## 6. Fail-closed verdicts: missing is not passing

| Verdict | Definition | Permitted disposition |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | required inputs inspected; declared predicate satisfied at stated scope; warrant and reference status adequate for that claim type | hand off as a bounded, advisory result |
| FAIL | an observed counterexample, mismatch, contradiction, or impermissible authority claim defeats the predicate | revise or withdraw the claim; preserve the failed case |
| UNKNOWN | required source, rule, translation, chronology, input, or execution is missing or unresolved | block endorsement of the affected claim and its dependents; name the next check `(to-verify)` |
| N/A | the predicate is demonstrably outside the declared scope | record the exclusion and rationale before review; cannot hide a dependency |

- Release rule: **every applicable required predicate must PASS**. FAIL and UNKNOWN both block endorsement, but remain different findings; successful unrelated checks cannot average them away.
- Missing evidence does not prove the opposite proposition. “No surviving garment found in the inspected corpus” cannot become “the culture wore no garments.”
- An inaccessible source or an unrun test is UNKNOWN, not PASS-with-caveat. Record completed checks separately from promised ones.
- Unknowns may travel to lore in an **issues-only or explicitly partial packet**; the packet must not advertise the blocked system as verified. Quarantine affected dependents and identify what remains usable.
- Explicit designer assumptions can support conditional internal tests, not historical validation. Do not relabel an unresolved I as D to erase its debt: retain the I/U record and identify the new, narrower design question.
- “Fail closed” protects the strength and authority of a statement; it does not prohibit deliberate ambiguity, conflicting traditions, invented metaphysics, or provisional exploration.

## 7. Coupling checks: inspect the edges, not only the components

| Edge | Re-runnable question | Typical hidden leap |
|---|---|---|
| language ↔ myth | do divine epithets and taboo substitutes derive from the declared naming/register rules? | an opaque name given a tidy etymology because the myth needs one |
| language ↔ symbol | does a rebus work in the specified spoken stage and dialect, not merely in the author's translation? | modern homophony projected into an older inscription |
| myth ↔ costume | who asserts that this vestment reenacts this narrative, in which rite and variant? | a decorative animal automatically made the wearer's god |
| symbol ↔ material | can the claimed carrier be made, obtained, maintained, or deliberately simulated? | rare dye assumed to entail a legal monopoly or one fixed meaning |
| era ↔ language | do borrowing, sound change, spelling, and name strata have compatible relative order? | script reform silently forcing an overnight spoken-language change |
| era ↔ costume | do fiber/cut, term, legal rule, and observed use belong to compatible intervals? | a later revival presented as unchanged ancestral dress |
| costume ↔ register | does a dress-reading scene specify the viewer's competence and the speech context? | a foreign observer's misreading treated as the code's objective meaning |

- Maintain a dependency graph: a failed dye-import date reopens dependent garment availability, loanword dating, and rank readings; it need not invalidate an unrelated phoneme inventory.
- No compulsory one-to-one alignment: one language may cross polities, one polity may use multiple languages/scripts, and one garment may carry competing readings. Scope the variants instead of manufacturing uniformity.
- Do not force diffusion to explain every resemblance. Shared signs or similar dress can arise by borrowing, convergence, chance, or an explicit fictional stipulation; test the asserted mechanism.

## 8. Era staging: several clocks, not one civilization ladder

| Clock | Record separately | Boundary test |
|---|---|---|
| event/use | proposed or evidenced time of manufacture, practice, or borrowing | can the claimed practice occur before its required material/contact channel? |
| witness | composition, performance, inscription, or observation date | is this a contemporary witness, retrospective account, or later reconstruction? |
| transmission | copy, translation, collection, restoration, or revival date | could a later wording, repaired garment, or editorial category explain the apparent early evidence? |
| reference/design | modern edition, catalog revision, corpus version, and proposal version | can a reviewer reproduce the same input and rule instead of silently using a newer one? |

- Use intervals and relative precedence where absolute dates are uncertain. Earliest surviving attestation bounds the record, not necessarily the invention of a practice.
- Test immediately before, at, and after the proposed era seam; specify calendar conversion, geographic lag, and which institutions recognize the change.
- An old liturgical form, inherited robe, or archaizing spelling is not automatically an error. Require a declared survival/revival mechanism and the appropriate register or ownership history.
- Never make oral → literate, bronze → iron, or draped → tailored into a universal social ranking. These are distinct variables, not a package that predicts myth, kinship, or naming.

## 9. Provenance calibration: reference knowledge has unequal resolution

| Evidence situation | Calibration | Required handling |
|---|---|---|
| locally scoped, well-supported description | usable at its documented resolution | preserve participant/group, date, medium, and exceptions; do not expand to a civilization-wide essence |
| competing reconstructions or interpretations | contested | name alternatives and what would distinguish them; test conditional designs without declaring the dispute solved |
| broad archetype, universal color dictionary, fixed-rate language clock used as proof | unsupported/discredited in that strong use; consult the routed calibration sections | retain only as labeled generative machinery, never as empirical backing |
| colonial administrator, missionary, conqueror, or hostile chronicler | situated; extraction, translation, and selection biases require scrutiny | distinguish quoted practice from authorial judgment; seek independent/contextual evidence without assuming all testimony is either transparent or worthless |
| surviving elite edicts, monuments, regalia, or luxury tombs | socially selective and survival-biased | do not generalize to commoners, rural practice, all genders, or everyday clothing without a bridge |
| thinly recorded oral practice or perishable costume | resolution limited; absence ambiguous | mark the tradition-specific claim `(to-verify)`; report what preservation, access, and collection can and cannot show |
| restricted or sacred knowledge in a living tradition | ethical hold where access/reuse is unresolved | respect customary restrictions; do not infer that digitization removes them; route permission/rights questions to the responsible owner |

- **Thin-knowledge declaration:** this overview does not establish competence in any particular tradition's language, ritual exegesis, garment construction, or chronicle textual history. A tradition-specific application needs its own checked sources and qualified review where necessary.
- When chronicle evidence is scarce, say so within a named corpus and search scope; do not invent missing priests, divine genealogies, garment names, or period dates to complete a comparison table.
- Do not conflate translation with original wording, a motif index with historical transmission, a linguistic family with an ethnicity, or a normative law with observed custom.
- Separate source identity, source access, passage support, interpretation, and reuse authority. A real author–year citation is necessary but insufficient when the passage does not support the proposition.
- If preparing a civilization comparison, use columns for **language family + writing; myth/pantheon variants; symbolic code; era model; costume/material signature; naming practices**. Each cell gets its own O/I/U tag, era, and locator; unknown cells remain unknown.

## 10. Manual execution and adversarial checks

1. Freeze the proposed input packet and the corpus rule version. Enumerate the claim IDs and distinguish empirical references from fictional D rules.
2. Route each claim through §5; record both the component predicates and the coupling-edge predicates in §7. Identify required versus genuinely inapplicable checks before testing.
3. Inspect each source locator and classify observations, warrants, assumptions, and gaps. Mark copied-source lineages so apparent agreement is not overcounted.
4. Fix expected results, tolerated ambiguity, and failure conditions before generating fresh test cases. Do not tune the rule after seeing the answer and call that prediction.
5. Execute derivations/readings and record actual results. For a qualitative inference, inspect a relevant alternative and document why it remains possible or is defeated.
6. Perform negative controls: introduce an illegal name segment, move a loan before contact, swap the viewer, or remove a material dependency. The corresponding predicate must reject or become UNKNOWN for the stated reason.
7. Test legitimate exceptions too: an explicitly imported cloth, scoped liturgical archaism, rival myth variant, or declared UNALIGNED symbol must not fail merely for being irregular.
8. Aggregate with §6, trace blocked dependents, and package findings for lore. A prose walkthrough is not an independent reviewer sign-off; name any reviewer and what they actually checked.
9. On revision, preserve the original failure, changed assumption/rule, and fresh result. Reopen dependent checks rather than retaining stale green verdicts.

## 11. Diagnostic fixture — invented test data, not a fictional world's canon

The following mini-system is deliberately artificial. Its premises are D test inputs, not observations of a real society or claims about any existing world.

| Fixture input | Declared premise | Expected constraint |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | no domestic silk production, no silk import, and no silk knowledge channel is supplied | local silk manufacture cannot be endorsed; a word for silk needs a separately declared knowledge channel |
| E1 | a supplier imports silk and the term /sara/ as a trade loan | silk availability and the loan are permissible from E1, not retrospectively proved for E0 |
| E2 | a sound law changes /s/ to /h/ in the productive vernacular; the ritual register preserves /s/ | vernacular /hara/, ritual /sara/; record which register any example uses |
| dress code | in E1–E2, a temple rule reserves imported silk sashes for officiants during one rite | legal eligibility is context-bound; actual compliance and a wearer's identity remain separate questions |
| symbol code | the sash's crane motif has one declared primary reading, “return,” with no rebus stipulated | a homophonic rebus needs an additional language-stage derivation; motif shape alone is insufficient |

| Test | Actual reasoning from the declared inputs | Expected verdict |
|---|---|---|
| E2 vernacular loan form = /hara/ | apply the declared sound law to E1 /sara/ | PASS — conditional internal derivation only |
| E2 ritual form = /sara/ | the declared register preserves /s/ | PASS — scoped exception, not an unexplained violation |
| E0 domestic manufacture of the E1 silk sash | E0 supplies neither silk production nor imports | FAIL — contradicts the declared material premises |
| E0 speakers must lack any silk word | lack of manufacturing does not establish lack of knowledge or vocabulary | UNKNOWN — knowledge/lexicon evidence missing `(to-verify)` |
| every silk-sash wearer is an officiant | rule supplies lawful eligibility, not absence of impersonation, borrowing, or disobedience | UNKNOWN — behavioral and identity evidence missing `(to-verify)` |
| crane motif proves the donor's religion was adopted | no transmission or religious-adoption premise exists | UNKNOWN — missing causal bridge `(to-verify)` |
| remove E1 while retaining the dated loan | derivation now lacks its adoption input | UNKNOWN — the dependent form loses endorsement; the law itself is not thereby disproved |

- Mutation check: replace proposed E2 vernacular /hara/ with /sara/ while keeping the same law and no exception; expected FAIL. Add an explicitly scoped liturgical exception and retest only that register, not every vernacular token.
- This fixture demonstrates decision logic, not a claimed historical finding, an automated test run, or external review of the lane.

## 12. Handoff boundary and stop rule

- Hand lore-shokunin the candidate rule tables, claim cards, evidence/dependency map, era/register versions, expected/actual test ledger, exceptions, and separated PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN findings.
- Preserve every `(to-verify)` inline at the affected claim and in the open-issues list; summaries must not drop uncertainty qualifiers or turn partial verification into blanket approval.
- Name the exact recipient and decision requested: adopt a candidate, choose among variants, commission a missing check, or decline it. An unanswered handoff remains unaccepted.
- Kotodama checks the derivation and evidential boundaries of its own proposed coupled systems; lore owns world-bible consistency verification and all canon decisions. Findings never authorize writing a sibling's files.
- Stop endorsement when an essential rule, warrant, source, or test is unresolved. Continue only with explicitly isolated reference work or conditional design branches; do not “complete the culture” by guessing.

## 13. What a worldbuilder can take — and provenance cautions

- Take the **claim card + routing table + dependency graph**: together they expose where a beautiful name, myth, symbol, or costume depends on an untested era or material assumption.
- Take **negative cases and legitimate exceptions together**: the system should reject mistakes without erasing contact, archaism, regional variance, contested belief, or deliberate misreading.
- Take **bounded uncertainty** as usable design material: rival chronicles, misleading dress, and lost vocabulary may remain unresolved without becoming unsupported narrator truth.
- Borrow mechanisms, not a real culture's identity package. A six-column civilization comparison is a map of differently evidenced systems, not a kit of interchangeable national essences.
- Provenance caution: Toulmin supplies argument structure, Wylie inferential discipline, Trouillot a power/silence audit, and UNESCO an ethical benchmark. None validates a specific tradition-specific claim on its own; those require checked passages, contexts, and explicit limits.
- Final invariant: **no observation → inference laundering; no unknown → fact laundering; no design → attestation laundering; no local PASS → canon laundering.**
