2. WHAT IT TEACHES (for the entry's scopes)
   This is the only row in the round that carries a working court: the commission of the special court, the
   statute it proceeded under, the indictment practice, the spectral evidence, and the defamation counter-suit
   that a accused person could bring and still lose in reputation. It is a 19th-century narrative history that
   quotes the 17th-century depositions and records verbatim, so it is a secondary text carrying primary
   quotations — worth flagging in the tier judgement.
   — accusation grammar: the grand-jury bill, the indictment read at the bar, the "complaint", the warrant.
   — proof technology: spectral evidence and the "afflicted children"; William Brattle's critique (below);
     the court's own explanation that the Devil removed the memory of contradicting witnesses.
   — confession: the examination records and the several "confessing witches".
   — slander law: the Godfrey defamation actions (below) — the counter-suit exists, is won in damages, and
     still leaves the plaintiff "rendred suspicious".
   — social functions: the village faction, the Parris salary dispute, and the property seizures.
   It does NOT give: the European statute text (it reports Hutchinson's account of the 1603 act), the Malleus,
   or any theological doctrine of witchcraft. Those are S914 and S916.

     Where Upham prints a deposition in quotation marks the inner text is primary; Upham's own sentences
     (E1, E2's first clause) are his interpretation. Keep the distinction in the entry.
   — There are no chapter headings in the e-text: cite volume + part + [i.N]/[ii.N] page marker. Do not invent
     a chapter number.
   — Underscores mark italics in this e-text (_in perpetuam rei memoriam_), and the 17th-century quotations
     keep original spelling ("agst.", "Sam.ll", "conceiue", "testmonyes", "rendred"). A quote lifted from
     those records must keep the spelling or it will not match.
   — The capture is not byte-identical (448 diff lines, all header/footer); cite it as corroboration of the
     quoted spans, not of the byte count.
