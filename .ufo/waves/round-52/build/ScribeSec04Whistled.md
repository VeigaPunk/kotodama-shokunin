## 4. Whistled speech

A whistled register re-encodes the local spoken language instead of listing fixed messages, and what survives depends on whether that language is tonal; the ecology-to-register coupling and the drum-and-whistle music links stay with `phonetics-phonology.md:197-199`, `music-instrument-lexicon.md:98-100`, `orality-formulaic-language.md:114,:124` and `sound-symbolism.md:121` (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2).

| System (language, community, place) | What the whistle keeps | What it drops | Distance and terrain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silbo Gomero (Spanish of La Gomera, Canary Islands; herders and farmers, taught in school since 1999) | vowel quality as whistle pitch (F2 to H1) and consonant place as two loci above and below the vowel range; voiced and voiceless as continuous and interrupted whistle (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-3) | most of the Spanish inventory: the classroom model has two vowel slots (contested — Meyer's measurements give four statistically distinct bands) and /o/ merges with /u/ (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1) | the island's ravines: messages carried over 2 to 3 km, heard as far as 8 km (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-3, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-9) |
| Whistled Turkish (Kuşköy, Görele valley, Black Sea; village and summer high-plateau herders) | eight vowels as eight pitch bands in four groups, disambiguated by vowel harmony; consonants as frequency and amplitude modulations, silence for stops (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-4, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-9) | band overlap between vowels, and lip-locked /p/, /b/ merging with /k/ in the whistle (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-4) | Black Sea mountains: the whistle takes over where speech fails, from about 40 to 100 m depending on the terrain; valleys carry it kilometres (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-5, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-7) |
| Mazatec (Oaxaca highlands; four recorded whistled varieties: Tenango, Eloxochitlan de Flores Magon, Huautla, San Mateo) | the tonal system itself: registers and lexically, morphologically and syntactically significant glides (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-10) | no transposition of consonants or vowels; only suprasegmental traits reach the whistle (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2) | Oaxaca mountain valleys: the same ease and intelligibility as speech, at close quarters and at a distance; distance figure (to-verify) (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-10, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-7) |
| Hmong (White Hmong; South-East Asia and its diaspora in Laos, Thailand, French Guiana) | tone melody: the whistle follows the spoken F0 contour, not the formants (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2) | nearly all vowel and consonant distinctions; only class-level alternation survives (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-2) | hill and mountain country of the Hmong dispersal; distance (to-verify) (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-6) |
| Chinantec (Sochiapam Chinantec, San Pedro Sochiápam, Oaxaca) | lexical tone: a rich tone system carries unpredictable sentences (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-6) | discourse length: whistles stay short, single-clause, with few repair initiations and few self-initiated repairs (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-6, NEW-ScribeSec07Encoding-2) | Oaxaca; the archive deposit was made with children of the village, so the register is learned young (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-8) |
| Siberian Yupik (not tonal) | a balance: the formant pull and stress intonation both count, so it sits between the segmental and tonal strategies (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-4) | the clean pitch scale a purely segmental whistle would give (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1) | Arctic; the corpus was collected with bilabial whistling in 2006; distance (to-verify) (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1) |

Decide the register before the lexicon: which tier the whistle carries — pitch standing for vowels (non-tonal), tone (tonal), or both (intermediate) — and which redundancy in the language will repair the loss (NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-1, NEW-ScribeSec04Whistled-5). Status **B** for the attested systems, **H** for this rule (`../verification/calibration-registry.md:35-39`).
