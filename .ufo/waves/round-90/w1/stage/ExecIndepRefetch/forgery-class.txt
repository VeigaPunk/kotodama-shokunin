FORGERY-CLASS DEMONSTRATION — round 90, seat ExecIndepRefetch
============================================================

What is shown
-------------
The round-90 advance-evidence grader has no network access: it reads committed
captures. A capture that a seat writes by hand and a quote that a seat invents
are therefore indistinguishable from a real fetch, and the counter accepts the
forgery with a full PASS. This is the round-89 survivor class, demonstrated
honestly rather than asserted. The only defence is independent re-fetch: a
second seat fetches the same URL itself, hashes its own bytes, and looks for the
claimed quote in what the server actually sent.

Conclusion
----------
1. The counter is a consistency checker, not a provenance checker. It proves a
   quote occurs in the capture it is given; it cannot prove the capture came
   from the URL. A seat that writes its own capture passes.
2. Independent re-fetch is the only defence. The defence is cheap and total:
   run 2 refuted every fabricated quote in one pass, because the fetched bytes
   did not contain the claimed spans.
3. Consequences for this round's C claim: every gate quote must be a tag-free
   substring of the raw bytes. Inline markup breaks the match — AIO renders
   "Eutychis, to the Highest (God), a vow." as two spans, so that sentence is
   absent from its own page capture, while "This is one of a number of imperial
   period dedications to Zeus Hypsistos" is present. WALS and ETCSL prose spans
   are clean; SIL glossary definitions are clean.
4. Capture hygiene that matters: save the body verbatim (no tag stripping, no
   re-wrapping), hash those exact bytes, and never let a capture be one of the
   graded files.
