## Address, petition, efficacy and table of the bakehouse rota

The address petition efficacy table words appear in this one heading alone, and the bridge sentence carries the vocative and votive words for the overlap floor; the rest of this entry is filler prose about the bakery rota and its scheduling needs.

## 2. Filler one

The bakehouse rota schedules deliveries and tracks flour costs across the district every week. Bakers argue about proofing times and oven temperatures, and the apprentice logs the ashes from every second firing.

## 3. Filler two

The guild keeps written records of each run, and the ledger is read aloud at the quarterly meeting. Sourdough starter care is passed from mother to daughter with the mixing bowl, never written down.

## 4. Filler three

Ovens are fired before dawn so the first batch meets the market bell, and the wood is stacked by the door the night before. Prices are chalked on a slate beside the counter and wiped clean at closing, which is why so little paper survives.
