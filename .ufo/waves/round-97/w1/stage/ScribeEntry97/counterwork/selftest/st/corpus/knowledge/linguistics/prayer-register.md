## address

The vocative formulae of prayer as a petitionary address register vary by rank, and the votive promises bind the petitioner to fixed and extempore speech with efficacy claims. A prayer opens by naming the deity and stating the petitioner, and the register carries the claim.

## petition

The petition states a want in a fixed order: address, reminder, request, promise. Votive promises and vocative formulae make the petitionary register legible to the deity and to the community that witnesses the rite of fixed and extempore speech.

## efficacy

A prayer claims efficacy through the address, the promise or the speaker. Efficacy claims in the petitionary register are graded in the culture, and extempore speech carries the weight of the petitioner when the fixed formula is absent.
