William Labov (1972:passim) in fact calls them “ritual insults” rather than verbal duels. Such categorization may have been a factor leading scholars to see all verbal duels as ritualized exchanges in general, and from there to understand them as “staged conflict,” or “not real conflict,” even when the duelists themselves stress improvisation and the conflictual element is quite strong.
