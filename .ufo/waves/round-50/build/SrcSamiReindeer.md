## Calibration bank material (draft items)

- **The evolutionary ladder.** FAO states the stage reading directly and refutes it:
  "Pastoralism was seen as an evolutionary stage in human history, a phase following
  hunting-gathering and leading to sedentarization and agriculture." Then, three sentences
  later, verbatim: "the increase of archaeological data and a more careful reading of the
  historical sources, especially from Asia, have demonstrated a more complex story" (both in
  the History and origins section). Entry rule: a herding culture is not "between" foraging
  and farming.
- **Herding ≠ full-time herding.** FAO's Kola Sámi case: small herds "leave the reindeer to run
  wild for much of the year", with fishing/hunting/trapping dominating in that period. This
  corroborates `finnic-sami.md:150` and kills the "herder with a staff, always walking" image.
- **Herding ≠ ethnic identity.** `finnic-sami.md:140` is the in-corpus guard; row 1 supplies the
  institutional reason (marks are held by persons and by the sameby, not by an ethnicity).
- **One animal, many regimes.** Rows 5 and 7 give two legal regimes (Norway: the earmark right
  is restricted by Sámi descent, a family herding history and participation in a siidaandel,
  with spouse/samboer and adoption exceptions; Sweden: a mark is registered for use in a named
  sameby, and a person may hold only one) — a fictional world should pick one gate and state
  it, not blend both.
- **Counts churn.** Row 3's 225 000–280 000 is a *range*, published with a page date; row 7's
  3.4 M is an undated order-of-magnitude. Never quote either as a precise census.
- **Agency summary ≠ court holding.** Row 2's burden-of-proof sentence is the agency's
  characterization. If the entry states it as law, it must be `(to-verify)` against a court
  source (e.g. the Nordmaling/Girjas line of cases) — this seat did not open case law.
- **Register as infrastructure.** Rows 1 and 6 show a mark system requires an authority, a
  register, an approval committee and a public lookup. A fictional earmark system with no
  registry is a different institution (customary only) and should be labelled as such.
