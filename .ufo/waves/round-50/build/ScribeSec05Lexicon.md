## 5. Herd lexicon: age, sex, colour, condition, ownership marks

**Master claim.** A herd vocabulary is a property index: each term answers what an owner must count, value, lend, recover or read at a distance (`linguistics/semantic-fields-lexicon-building.md:70`). Elaborate where the herd is read daily, not where it is merely large (`linguistics/semantics-pragmatics.md:13`).

| Dimension | Attested pattern (opened) | Design rule for the conlang field |
|---|---|---|
| Colour + distribution | Nuer: "ten principal colour terms", base colour plus where it sits on the body; "at least twenty-seven" combinations [SRC: EP1940] | 8–10 base roots + a distribution affix set; a name is base + placement, never a flat list |
| Age × sex | Nuer prefixes: tut (bull), yang (cow), thak (ox), nac (heifer), ruath (male calf), dou (female calf), kol (calf not yet grazing) [SRC: EP1940] | Age × sex as a prefix grid, not a dozen roots |
| Condition, ornament | Ox-name elements: bell and tassel terms, "fatness", "shining whiteness" [SRC: EP1940] | Condition is a name-element slot, not a grade scale |
