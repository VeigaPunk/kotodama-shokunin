## 9. What a worldbuilder does with this

Use the rows as conditional design prompts, not prescriptions. Select an option, combine alternatives, or specify exceptions for a named setting.
<!-- BULLETS 9 -->

| Worldbuilder choice | Possible consequence for dialogue | Possible consequence for social structure |
|---|---|---|
| Children are corrected openly | Scenes may include explicit repair, evaluation, or public restatement. | Make the correction-giver's authority and the learner's public standing design variables. |
| Correction is private or indirect | Put reformulation, delayed response, or private practice in the scene instead of public correction. | Decide who may intervene and how feedback moves between participants. |
| Children choose among names, titles, or kin terms for adults | Specify which address form each speaker uses in each defined relationship and setting. | Define which relations and settings license each form, including exceptions. |
| A child uses more than one language or variety | Mark choices, shifts, and interpretation only as the fictional design defines them. | Decide who can access, understand, or value each form and where those relations apply. |
| Children are invited to tell or retell shared stories | Choose who speaks, who responds, and what counts as an acceptable retelling in the scene. | Decide how participation and authority are distributed for that activity. |
