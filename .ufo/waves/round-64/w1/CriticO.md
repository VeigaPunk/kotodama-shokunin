### Mechanism and impacted axes

- `reports/round-64/accept-o.py:1037-1040` unconditionally replaces `urljoin(current, location.strip())` with `urljoin(current, "/" + location.strip())`. It changes scheme-relative and absolute locations as well as slashless paths. For example, an executed pure-stdlib probe showed `//127.0.0.1:41002/secret` resolving normally to that other authority but under m5 to `http://127.0.0.1:41001/127.0.0.1:41002/secret` at the original authority. This produces UNREACHABLE/2 rather than the fixture's required REFUSED/1.
- Lines 1076-1091 launch each child with `--only` its pins, then derive `failed` only from `pinned_lines`. Equality with the pins cannot expose failures of skipped legs. Revision 3's claim to require the observed failure-leg set to equal the pins is therefore too strong.
- **Operability:** the executable exact-isolation control is unsound, despite genuine pinned kills. **Calibration:** claiming exactness from this receipt overstates observed evidence. **Coverage:** no corpus coverage regression was tested or alleged; C's entry/row work is outside this review.
