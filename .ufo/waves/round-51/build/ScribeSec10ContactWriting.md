```
## 10. Contact, codes and writing

| System | What it is | What it is not | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manual alphabet / fingerspelling | conventionalized handshapes represent letters of a spoken alphabet; produced sequentially (NEW-6) | a language; letter-families differ — one-handed (ASL, LIS, ISL) vs two-handed BANZSL (BSL/Auslan/NZSL) (NEW-6) | pick one family per deaf-community language; reserve fingerspelling for names, new terms, borrowings |
| Initialized signs, mouthing | letter-based signs — a sign's first letter with a circular movement — and co-occurring mouthing, as contact traces (NEW-7) | evidence that signed grammar mirrors spoken word order (NEW-7) | assign them to name, school and newest registers; keep core grammar free of them |
| Contact signing | improvised signing between signers of different national sign languages (NEW-2) | a fixed language (NEW-3); a pidgin or creole (NEW-9) | fits fairs, federations, pilgrimages; expect local, unstable varieties |
| International Sign | WFD's meeting/interpreting register; deaf people choose it alongside national sign languages (NEW-1); contact signing whose conventionalization is partial and that has no native users (NEW-3) | a universal sign language or a substitute for a national sign language (NEW-1) | shared-stage occasions only; never a home language (NEW-3) |
| Signed versions of spoken languages | deliberately designed visual codes for a spoken language — e.g. Signed English (14 sign markers) arranges a borrowed sign vocabulary in English word order (NEW-8) | naturally acquired sign languages; not ASL-English bilingual education (NEW-8) | only if your world has a deaf-education policy story; keep codes distinct from languages |
| Notation and writing | Stokoe-lineage transcription (NEW-4); HamNoSys, a phonetic-style transcription, international, not tied to any national fingerspelling (NEW-4); SignWriting writes sign languages directly (NEW-5) | scripts of a spoken language — they record manual-visual form, not sound (`writing-systems.md:21`) | a literate deaf community writes SignWriting; analysts transcribe with HamNoSys; never conflate notation with a language |
```
