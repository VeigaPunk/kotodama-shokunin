```
## 9. Alternate sign languages: signing by hearing people

Signing by hearing people is a design slot wherever speech is banned, blocked or impractical: a manual register beside the spoken one (NEW-01, NEW-06), never a deficient substitute.

| System | Trigger | Who signs | Relation to the spoken language | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aboriginal Australian sign (Warlpiri) | mourning speech restrictions — names of the dead (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:75`) | hearing signers, incl. bereaved women `(to-verify)` | alternate system used instead of speech where speech is disallowed (NEW-01, NEW-06, NEW-08) | build a lexicon-swap register; the speech ban stays owned at `politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:75,:77` |
| Plains Indian Sign Talk (PST) | intertribal contact: trade and alliances across many spoken languages (NEW-02) | signers across nations — hearing and Deaf (NEW-02) | lingua franca crossing spoken-language barriers (NEW-02; 1930 council film NEW-04, NEW-05, NEW-07) | a shared manual convention for contact zones; the film record archives it (NEW-04) |
| Monastic signs (Cluny, c. 900–1200) | vows of silence under a religious rule (NEW-03) | monks bound by the rule (NEW-03) | replaces speech in the cloister for necessary communication; deliberately restricted (NEW-03) | a small, taught, policed inventory — the template for fictional silent orders |
| Occupational signing in noise | working environments where speech fails | workers in the trade | supplements or overrides speech in the noisy channel | a domain-limited work shorthand — **H**, `(to-verify)` for attested cases |

Design rule: set the trigger first — ritual ban, contact, rule, or channel — it fixes who signs, what the register covers, and its relation to speech.
Calibration: rows rest on opened sources (**B**) or are marked `(to-verify)`; the occupational row is the lane's heuristic (**H**, `calibration-registry.md:35-39`).
The speech-ban trigger is the taboo machinery of `politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:75` (names of the dead; occupational bans) and `:101` (fossil substitutes): a manual register is what a culture signs when speech is charged.
Terminology: sign languages, signers, Deaf communities — never "deaf-mute", "dumb", or "hearing-impaired".
```
