## 0. Classification vocabulary

| class | meaning |
|---|---|
| **owns** | the file's `- topic:`/section claims the surface as its subject |
| **mentions** | the surface appears as an attribute, example or consequence of a system the file does own |
| **hands-off** | the file states a division of labour that explicitly leaves the surface elsewhere |

Measured result: **0 owns, 7 mentions, 3 hands-off** across the ten named files — the classes are
mutually exclusive here. See `gap-census.md` §4 for the structural proof (6 headings name it,
0 own it).

## 1. The ten named files

### 1.1 `linguistics/kinship-terminology.md` — **mentions** (strongest partial owner)

Owning lines:

```
:80  ## 5. Affinal logic: the marriage rule is usually IN the lexicon
:82  - Two classical schools: **descent** theory (lineages reproduce themselves; Radcliffe-Brown & Forde 1950)
     vs **alliance** theory (lineages reproduce *through* exchanging brides; Lévi-Strauss 1949). Both are
     half-right; modern work uses both locally and models the household instead where descent fails
     (Carsten & Hugh-Jones 1995)
:88  - Wife-giver/wife-taker asymmetry: systems that remember who gives and who takes mark it asymmetrically
     in terms and deference; the debt outlives the wedding. Strong material for feuds and hostage-marriages.
:138 8. Hand off to lore-shokunin: term tables, rules, and strata as reference options; every to-verify
     flagged; canon decisions remain theirs per lane rules.
```

What it owns: **which marriage rule a lexicon encodes**, and the terminological signature of
wife-giver/wife-taker asymmetry. What it does *not* own: the transfer itself, the estate, the
ceremony, the parties' obligations, the breach. The new entry must cite `:82` and `:88` for the
lexical trace and **not** re-derive Dravidian/Arab/Sinitic marriageability (that whole material is
`kinship-terminology.md` §5, lines 80–102).

This is the one file where the boundary is genuinely contested: `:88` says "the debt outlives the
wedding" — a sentence about the institution, written inside the lexicon file. The new entry should
either (a) adopt it as the pointer that justifies the entry, or (b) restate it *once* with the
citation and say the lexicon file keeps the terms. It must not do both silently.
