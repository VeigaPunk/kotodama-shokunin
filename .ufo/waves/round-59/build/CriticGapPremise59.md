## 4. The Calibration Traps the Entry Must Mark (`§11` & `§12`)

The new entry must explicitly flag and calibrate **eight high-frequency worldbuilding and historiographical traps**:

1. **Trap 1 — A universal "evil eye" everywhere (`R` as universal / `B` as circum-Mediterranean & West/South Asian complex):**
   - *The trap:* Treating the "evil eye" (malevolent/envious gaze causing drying, sickness, or ruin, answered by blue beads or eye motifs) as an automatic human universal present in every culture from East Asia to pre-Columbian America.
   - *The calibration:* While envy and witchcraft anxieties are widespread, the specific **evil-eye complex** (*baskania*, *fascinatio*, *ʿayn*, *mati*, *nazar*, *malocchio*) has a documented historical geography centered in the Mediterranean, Near East, North Africa, South Asia, and their contact/colonial diasporas (as Dundes and Elliott document). In other traditions (e.g., Egyptian *wedjat* as the restored whole eye of Horus, `egypt-ancient.md:96`, or Ethiopian scroll eyes as the watchful gaze of God/angels), an eye image has a *different* theology. Never equate every eye motif with the Mediterranean evil-eye complex.
2. **Trap 2 — Frazer's sympathetic magic (similarity + contact) as a universal natural law (`R` as explanatory law / `C` as etic heuristic):**
   - *The trap:* Building a fictional amulet system as if Frazer's *Golden Bough* laws of homeopathic (like produces like) and contagious (once in contact, always in contact) magic (`myth-ritual-theories.md:32-34`, `purity-pollution-boundary-systems.md:139`) were an unmediated universal physics or a "primitive stage" prior to religion.
   - *The calibration:* Frazer separated "magic" (coercive, primitive pseudo-science) from "religion" (supplicatory prayer) on an evolutionary ladder that modern scholarship rejects. In actual historical objects — Ketef Hinnom's priestly blessing (`israel-ancient-hebrew.md:108`), Byzantine *phylaktēria*, Islamic Qur'anic *taʿwīdh*, Japanese *omamori* — supplication, divine command, liturgical scripture, and material contact operate **inside** institutional religion, not prior to or outside it.
3. **Trap 3 — "Pagan survival" readings of Christian, Islamic, Jewish, or Buddhist protective practices (`R` / `C`):**
   - *The trap:* Assuming that whenever a medieval European peasant carves a mark on a beam, wears a saint's medal, or recites a healing charm, or whenever a Muslim wears a *ḥijāb* or a Buddhist wears an amulet, it is a "thinly disguised pagan survival" (`ritual-liminality.md:131`).
   - *The calibration:* Most medieval and early modern protective marks (e.g., Marian `M` / `AMR` monograms, `IHS`, cross-hatching) and charms (historiolae invoking Christ, Mary, St. Blasius, or the Three Kings) were conceived and practiced by devout Christians using liturgical logic. Even where pre-Christian motifs or dual moulds exist (`migration-founder-effects.md:147`), syncretism and re-keying change the meaning (`symbolism-consistency-rules.md:106`). Write in-world "pagan survival" claims as an **in-world reformer's polemical accusation** or an **antiquarian's pet theory**, never as the entry's flat historical fact.
4. **Trap 4 — Romanticised "witch-mark" folklore and over-reading every scratch on old timber (`C` / `R`):**
   - *The trap:* Calling every compass-drawn daisy wheel (hexafoil), burn mark, or scratch on a medieval/early modern timber building a "witch mark" carved in terror during witch trials, or treating every geometric mark as an occult sigil.
   - *The calibration:* Heritage and archaeological surveys (e.g., Historic England / museum studies of ritual protection marks) note that (a) "witch mark" is a popular modern umbrella term for **apotropaic or ritual protection marks** aimed at general misfortune, demons, fire, and lightning as well as maleficium; (b) compass-drawn hexafoils and carpenter's setting-out marks or candle-scorch tests can overlap visually and require **contextual placement evidence** (clustering around thresholds, lintels, hearths, windows) and experimental checking (deliberate gouging of char vs accidental taper burns); and (c) Marian invocations are devotional protection, not witchcraft.
5. **Trap 5 — Closed or living religious practices treated as exotic costume or generic "magic items" (`RED LINE`):**
   - *The trap:* Treating living sacred practices — Jewish *mezuzot* and *tefillin* (`ritual-garments.md:36`), Japanese shrine *omamori* and *ofuda*, Islamic Qur'anic *taʿwīdh*, Amazigh/MENA *khamsa*/*hamsa* (`amazigh-berber.md:100`), Kongo *minkisi* (`kongo-kingdom.md:83,89,144`), or Indigenous medicine bundles — as interchangeable fantasy "loot drops," decorative costume props, or "primitive fetishes."
   - *The calibration:* Per `verification/real-world-inspiration-boundaries.md:91-108` and `verification/myth-consistency-gate.md:34`, living traditions are described through their documented institutional rules and public museum/community records, never mined for restricted ritual detail or flattened by colonial "fetish" terminology (`kongo-kingdom.md:144`). Moreover, within living traditions, practitioners themselves distinguish a commandment (*mitzvah*) or devotional blessing from "magic" or "superstition" — the worldbuilder must model that internal boundary (`binding-speech-complex.md:164`).
6. **Trap 6 — Archaeological survivorship bias (the "stone-and-metal illusion", `R`):**
   - *The trap:* Designing a fictional culture where all amulets are carved gemstones and cast gold/bronze pendants because museum cases are full of gems and metal.
   - *The calibration:* Cross-referencing `mineral-stone-symbolism.md:178` and `writing-media-surface-economy.md:83-96`, stone, faience, and metal survive in the ground while the actual everyday majority — knotted threads, herbal sachets, chalked door marks, paper slips, wood tallies, and wax — rots or burns. A realistic world bible pairs a durable elite/funerary register with a perishable vernacular register.
7. **Trap 7 — "Readable text = literate wearer" (`R`):**
   - *The trap:* Inferring population-wide literacy from widespread inscribed amulets, or conversely assuming an illiterate person would not buy a written charm (`israel-ancient-hebrew.md:31`, `japan-medieval-sengoku.md:115`).
   - *The calibration:* In protective script economies, the text is frequently addressed to **demons, angels, or gods**, not to human passers-by — which is why texts are rolled tight inside soldered tubes, written in spiral/pseudo-script or *charaktēres*, or sewn inside cloth pouches that the wearer is forbidden to open.
8. **Trap 8 — Single-meaning iconographic dictionaries (`R`):**
   - *The trap:* Assuming every scarab is purely a funerary amulet (`egypt-ancient.md:97` notes scarabs intersect seal, phonetic sign, and amulet), every hand pendant has one pan-Mediterranean meaning (`amazigh-berber.md:100`), or every portable carving on Rapa Nui is an amulet (`rapa-nui.md:161`).
   - *The calibration:* Require `form + material + placement + inscription + user/occasion` before assigning a protective reading (`myth-consistency-gate.md:119`).
