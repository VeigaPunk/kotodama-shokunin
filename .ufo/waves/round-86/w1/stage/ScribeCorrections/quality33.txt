33. **A quote hit does not certify its claimed paragraph position.**

A source-row locator claiming opening/first/second article paragraph must identify the containing article paragraph and check the asserted ordinal separately from finding the quote. Count scoped article paragraphs, not page navigation, headings, captions or boilerplate; state that scope. Whitespace folding and nested inline markup must not turn a found span into an assumed first-paragraph verdict. If the span is absent or occurs in multiple eligible paragraphs, do not certify a unique ordinal.
