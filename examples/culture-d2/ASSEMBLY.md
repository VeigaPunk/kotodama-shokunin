# D2 round-3 assembly and measured gate verdicts

Non-canon design drill. This is a gate packet, not an accepted culture or a lore handoff. Existing design contradictions are retained, not repaired to make gates green. No git commit was made and no `corpus/knowledge/` file was edited.

## Reproduce the packet

From this repository:

```sh
python3 tools/assemble-d2.py
python3 tools/assemble-d2.py --check
```

The stdlib-only merger resolves paths from its own location, extracts named top-level blocks, checks ledger record types/required fields/unique IDs, and emits one deterministic YAML-subset document. `--check` compares the generated bytes without writing. Packet SHA-256 for the final runs:

## Authored naming component

`components/13-naming.yml` supplies personal, clan, place and deity-epithet slots, assignment/life-stage rules, eight root-linked etymologies, and three rejected candidates with institutional reasons:

- Persons: **sunel** = su L-08 + nel L-02, wind-hand; **banwal** = ban L-03 + wal L-29, bone-oath.
- Clans: **sulas** = su L-08 + las L-04, wind-lineage; **mirlas** = mir L-34 + las L-04, fleece-lineage.
- Places: **kolsin** = kol L-17 + sin L-10, pass-snow; **tolmir** = tol L-14 + mir L-34, low-ground fleece.
- Proposed cult addresses: **belwal** = bel L-13 + wal L-29, height-oath (proposed bearer PAN-04); **hursu** = hur L-09 + su L-08, storm-wind (proposed bearer PAN-02). These are not new gods or adopted aliases; 06's theonyms remain unchanged.

Every accepted name is two syllables of `(C)V(C)`, with codas only **n/l/r/s**, a single onset at most, no geminate, no hiatus, and no segment outside 02's fifteen consonants and five vowels. All use the default initial pitch-accent peak, not a newly invented honor shift. The component follows `corpus/knowledge/linguistics/naming-systems.md` and cites 02's P/AC rules and 05's roots. All three rejected candidates—suwal, tolsa and sinlas—are also phonotactically legal; their rejection concerns sacred-formula appropriation, death avoidance, and falsely claimed hereditary affiliation respectively.

Honor is a license to call for a lineage, not a new affix. Public wind-reached use of a deceased person's name adopts L-32's four-grass-season avoidance and existing sanction, explicitly flagging the indefinite avoidance in 06/07 as unresolved. This is an invented institution, not real-world evidence or supernatural fact.

Independent self-checks confirmed all 11 candidate strings' inventory membership, syllable boundaries and absence of geminates; each accepted name's two bases exists in the 40-record lexicon and concatenates to the delivered spelling. This is **not** a non-author or cross-cultural clearance. The eight `DERIV-HOLD`s below are retained because 03 does not expose the gate's executable derivation adapter.

The costume gate accepts numeric CE-comparable boundaries there and returned **exit 2**, `G-DATA: expected a number, got None`. Strings such as D1 would also be the wrong type. ## Limits and outstanding reviews

- This is not a general YAML implementation. The merger reuses the existing costume gate's stdlib subset parser, first slicing only named top-level blocks. It does not support arbitrary tags, anchors, aliases or multi-document streams, and does not parse every component's prose. Comments and original formatting are not copied. Independent gate-reader comparisons check selected record values, not full YAML conformance.
- No guessed semantics: structured symbol domains stay mappings; missing media are not extracted heuristically from descriptive text. Provisional theonyms are not rewritten using newer roots. The source's n/l/r/s and no-cluster prose are the only phonology schema transcriptions.
- Rank rules in 12 are prose-rich WR/TR/LO records, not executable `rank_rules`; they were **not** translated. The material inventory in 10 is not unioned with 11's costume-facing subset. Silk versus broadcloth, pack-ox down versus hair cordage, and felt-grade mappings remain owner/integrator questions. These omissions narrow the gate result and are not a full-system pass.
- 04 orthography, 14 kin-address and 15 pragmatics were not merged into the eight requested base ledgers. Script accent-mark conflicts, connected-use names, kin up/down-call forms and pragmatic licenses still need integration. 07's proposed names and lexemes remain proposals, not adopted 05/13 inventory.
- Era names, stage tags, 08's E1-E4 local labels, and 12's era-1..3 are not treated as interchangeable. Numeric CE dates are unavailable. No dated costume process is claimed, so removing the inappropriate numeric field does not certify any absolute anachronism check.
- Missing reviews: `reviews/03-morphosyntax.md` and `reviews/13-naming.md` explicitly record **ABSENT — component not delivered** from their earlier review windows; neither reviews the delivered morphology or these new names. Those historical reviews were left unchanged. Non-author review of the final integrated packet, D1 ledger comparison, bounded real-language/cultural/legal sweep, reader-channel/whistle/connected-address trials, astronomical latitude/date verification and source scholarship checks remain outstanding. No self-check is represented as a promotion or canon approval.
- The requested round-3 TSV/output layout is preserved, with byte-identical input copies added for integrity. It is **not** the committed per-gate `expected.json` layout prescribed by the corpus frozen-run standard. The user prohibited commits; no committed-baseline/two-evidences conformance claim is made. Only the five packet gates and two provenance inputs requested here are the measured task verdicts; `handoff-packet-check` was not requested or run.
