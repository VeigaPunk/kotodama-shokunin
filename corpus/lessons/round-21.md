## Gate

### Rule

A claim's `before` and `after_required` fields state what is wanted; they do not show that it happened. A worker's green summary, a passing sub-check, a matching inventory count, or the mere presence of an artifact is likewise insufficient by itself. Admit only the part of a claim that a dated receipt actually measures against the named criterion. Bind that reading to its command and input revision, preserve the declared and observed values separately, and disposition each mismatch explicitly. If a final receipt is absent, the gate is pending—not passed.

Keep claim-level readings distinct from the round-level result. A receipt may pass one claim while another fails, or accept a documented declared-versus-live delta without changing the declared value into a measured one. A later recount belongs to its own revision and time; it neither rewrites the earlier receipt nor proves the earlier reading was false at its original stamp.

## Closeout rule

Before any claim is admitted, read the assigned round-21 receipt and check that each declared criterion has its own corresponding measured output, command, date, and revision. Confirm every discrepancy is either reproduced or given a named, evidenced disposition. If the receipt or a required measurement is missing, state the gap and leave the claim open.
