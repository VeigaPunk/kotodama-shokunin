- Script readability, affiliation, spoken use and institutional register
  are different questions. The Aegean, Hittite and Kush fragments require
  partial-scope reconciliation rather than one bundled full confirmation.
- An Egyptian pleated linen object can challenge an exclusive New Kingdom
  dating rule without giving a complete cutting/pleating reconstruction.
  Keep the museum's attribution and independent re-dating separate.
- Timna's purple identification supports a particular material and date;
  absence of a universal wearing law in that paper is not proof that no
  restriction ever existed. A workshop does not supply a native garment name.

  earlier access failure for that witness without making Old Persian the
  language of all imperial subjects or verifying all Behistun versions.

  witnesses, not excavated universal wardrobes. Keep surfaces, worn items,
  named timing options and translator emendations separately attributable.

  labels. Publisher-deposited abstract prose is content; its surrounding
  bibliographic metadata is not. An archive wildcard is not an exact capture.
