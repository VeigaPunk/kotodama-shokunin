## 1. What a creation type actually decides

- A cosmogony is the culture's template for what "making" is. Whatever happened "at the start" is what competent making looks like forever after. Malinowski's charter theory (1926) is the weak, safely usable version — origin stories license present arrangements. Eliade's strong version (every ritual reenacts the cosmogony; renewal repeats creation) is design language, not ethnographic fact (§12).
- The five variables every creation story fixes, which are the actual worldbuilding levers: (a) raw material — nothing, formless waters, a body, an egg, land-under-water, speech; (b) agency — word, thought, combat, sex/separation, climbing, diving, hatching; (c) cost — who pays (a victim, a mother's death, the divers' lives, nobody); (d) time-shape — linear from a start, cyclical, or a spiral of worlds; (e) direction — order as progress toward something, or decay from a lost fullness.
- Long's fifth type "creation from chaos" is folded here into §7 (chaos as matter) and §8 (chaos as enemy): chaos-as-stuff versus chaos-as-opponent is the distinction that changes institutions.
- Typology is an etic sorting bin, not a genealogy: type-sharing does not mean historical relatedness. Matched motif detail is the only comparison that counts (comparative-frameworks.md §7).

## 3. Earth-diver

- Mechanism: primeval waters cover everything; a creator (often matrifocal — Sky Woman falls from a sky-world, or is sent) commissions animals to dive for mud; the successful diver, often dead or spent, is honored; earth grows from the handful on the diver's body or on a floating back.
- Exemplars: Haudenosaunee — Sky Woman falls through the uprooted sky-tree, birds set her on the turtle's back, the muskrat's dive founds the land (Parker 1923); Yoruba — Obatala descends the chain with sand, a hen, and a palm nut; drunk on palm wine he fails, and Oduduwa completes the work at Ile-Ife, the world's navel (Idowu 1962); the type is the most widely distributed of all creation types in North America and extends through Siberia and Finno-Ugric lands (Dundes 1962; Holmberg 1927).
- Implied cosmology: the world is an artifact built from scarce material over a hostile medium; land is fragile, additive, and can always be lost back to the water.
- Implied values: interspecies reciprocity (the founder-animals sacrificed); humility of the creator who needs help; the first act of the polity is a debt to nonhumans.
- Craft hooks: land-tenure as a gratitude economy toward the diving species; guest-right owed to the muskrat's kin; famine or flood read as breach of the first reciprocity. Language coupling: species-names and place-names record the dive; boasting about one's contribution is the tabooed speech act ("credit for the dive" belongs to the diver, not the commissioner).

## 4. Emergence

- Mechanism: beings climb from dark womb-worlds upward through successive strata into this one, assisted by digger-guides, carrying the failures of earlier worlds with them.
- Exemplars: Diné — four worlds (black, blue, yellow, glittering); quarrels and thefts force each climb; Badger and Locust dig the way out; Coyote's thefts trigger the final flood (Zolbrod 1984; Reichard 1950); Hopi — emergence through the sipapuni, Spider Grandmother guides, Maasaw offers ears of corn, and the Hopi choose the shortest, hence this world and its stewardship (Courlander 1971); Zuni — four womb-caves (Cushing 1896, unreliable narrator, §12).
- Implied cosmology: a vertical multiverse; this world is neither first nor final nor best; existence is graduated fit, not a fall from perfection.
- Implied values: patience and remediation over conquest; the migration route is the charter of legitimacy (route = title deed); disease ideology — each world leaves residue, and ritual (Navajo chantway logic, Reichard 1950) repairs balance rather than defeating enemies.
- Craft hooks: the emergence hole is architecture (the kiva sipapu) and pilgrimage geography; sin = importing an upper-world disease downward; sacralized professions: guides, diggers, midwives. Calibration: "Hopi Fifth-World apocalypse" prophecy kitsch is a modern anglophone invention (Geertz 1994) — never hand it to a world bible as Hopi belief.

## 5. World-parent (union-separation and dismemberment)

- Two sub-mechanisms. (a) Union-separation: a primordial couple locked in embrace; children pry them apart; sky and earth are persons. (b) Dismemberment: a primeval body is cut up; the parts become the world.
- Exemplars (a): Māori — Ranginui and Papatūānuku separated by Tāne, sinews cut; Tāwhirimātea's storms are grief, and weather is a family quarrel (Grey 1855; Orbell 1995); Japanese — Izanagi and Izanami stir the brine with the jeweled spear, drops form Onogoro, the pair begets the islands and gods; Izanami dies at fire-birth, the Yomi descent follows, and Izanagi's misogi purification begets Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi, and Susanoo (Philippi 1968).
- Exemplars (b): Vedic — Purusha's eye becomes the sun, mind the moon, head the sky, feet the earth; mouth, arms, thighs, feet become the four varnas (RV 10.90); Norse — Ymir's flesh, blood, bones, skull, and brain become earth, sea, mountains, sky, clouds (Faulkes 1987; Larrington, Grímnismál 40–41, Völuspá 3); Chinese — Pangu's body becomes mountains, rivers, wind, and stars (Birrell 1993; first attested only 3rd c. CE, §12).
- Implied cosmology: the cosmos is a kinship body; order is differentiated roles from one body; the world is a corpse or a family estate, never raw material.
- Implied values: lineage over contract; ancestors as structural powers, not sentiment; the dismembered victim is the culture's suppressed charter — who is cut up and who profits is the ideology map (Purusha encodes caste literally).
- Craft hooks: provinces named for organs ("the Shoulder" range); tax as "recomposing" the body; rebellion framed as hemorrhage; the Japanese pattern makes purification cosmogonic work — handling pollution continues creation (couples politeness-taboo). Language coupling: priest-genealogists recite the cosmos as a family tree; Māori whakapapa recitation is cosmogony performed as genealogy (couples orality-formulaic-language).

## 6. Cosmic egg

- Mechanism: the world hatches from, or is divided out of, an egg or embryo; structure is latent and unfolds, not fought for or built.
- Exemplars: Vedic — Hiranyagarbha, the golden embryo, arises as lord of all (RV 10.121); the Self-existent desiring to create produces waters, then a golden egg in which he himself is born, and divides it into heaven and earth (Manu 1.5–16, Olivelle 2005); Egyptian — the Hermopolitan Ogdoad's egg laid on the primeval mound (Lichtheim I; Frankfort 1946); Orphic — Phanes bursts from the world-egg (West 1983; the Derveni papyrus, Betegh 2004; Aristophanes parodies it in Birds 693ff — the parody proves the doctrine's currency); Finnish — a teal nests on Ilmatar's knee; the broken shells become earth and sky (Kalevala runo 1, Magoun 1963); Chinese — Pangu within the chaos-egg (Birrell 1993); Mangaia — Vatea born from the primordial mother's egg of flesh (Gill 1876).
- Implied cosmology: the cosmos is an organism, not an artifact; macrocosm-microcosm correspondence (the human is a small egg); astrological determinism fits naturally; the frontier is a shell.
- Implied values: destiny over choice; embryological and seasonal ritual logic; analogic magic — what works on the small works on the great.
- Craft hooks: birth-star institutions; "world-age" calendars that treat cosmic time as gestation; an explorer culture's dread of piercing the shell; the sovereign as hatcher. Calibration: the Kalevala is Lönnrot's 19th-c. redaction of authentic oral runos — a constructed epic, not received scripture; Dogon "egg of the world" material rests on Griaule's contested ethnography and the Sirius claim is pseudoarchaeology-adjacent — do not use (van Beek 1991).

## 7. Ex nihilo

- Mechanism: creation by will or decree with no pre-existing material; very often by word.
- Exemplars: Genesis 1 (see calibration); Egyptian — Ptah creates through heart (thought) and tongue (command), the limbs executing what the tongue orders (Memphite Theology, Shabaka Stone, Lichtheim I); Amun the hidden one who self-generates (New Kingdom hymns, Lichtheim II); Qur'anic kun — "Be, and it is" (2:117; 36:82); Augustine — time itself is created with the world (Confessions XI.30; City of God XI.6); Maya — Heart of Sky speaks "let it be so" and earth appears, but the first spoken creations fail twice — mud people dissolve, wooden people are destroyed — before maize people succeed (Popol Vuh, Tedlock 1985).
- Implied cosmology: absolute creator/world distinction; being is gratuitous (nothing was owed anything); linear time with a start; will and language are ontologically primary.
- Implied values: obedience, covenant, gratitude; revelation outranks custom; whoever may legitimately speak for the creator holds political power — scribes, prophets, jurists.
- Craft hooks: a full language politics — who may utter the creation-words; blasphemy as cosmic rather than civil crime; decree-verbs that resist translation; failed creations as natural-history lore (fossils, ruins, extinct species); anti-idolatry aesthetics in art and costume. Calibration — the live debate: whether Genesis 1 is ex nihilo or formation from pre-existing waters (tehom, kin to Tiamat) is contested; explicit ex nihilo doctrine crystallizes in Hellenistic-era Judaism and patristic thought, with 2 Maccabees 7:28 as the classic prooftext (May 1994; Levenson 1988). Write ancient-feeling fictional traditions as formation-from-something unless the culture has a late philosophical school to argue otherwise.

## 8. Creation by combat (chaoskampf)

- Mechanism: order is won by defeating a chaos figure — usually the sea or a dragon; the victor becomes king; the cosmos is a victory that must be held.
- Exemplars: Babylonian — Marduk kills and splits Tiamat, heaven from one half, earth from the other, humanity from Kingu's blood to serve the gods, the calendar fixed as an act of dominion, the Fifty Names bestowed (ANET, pp. 60–72; Dalley 1989); Vedic — Indra slays the dragon Vritra lying on the mountain and releases the waters (RV 1.32, O'Flaherty 1981); Ugaritic — Baal defeats Yam and the seven-headed Lotan (Smith 1994); Greek — Zeus defeats Typhoeus after two succession wars (West 1966, Theogony 820ff); Egyptian — Ra fights Apophis nightly, so re-creation happens every dawn (Frankfort 1946); Norse — Thor and Jormungandr fall at Ragnarök, after which the earth rises green again (Larrington, Völuspá).
- Term: chaoskampf was coined by Gunkel (1895); the biblical sea echoes — crushed dragon heads, Leviathan fleeing (Ps 74:13–17; Isa 27:1) — are its best-documented case (Day 1985).
- Implied cosmology: order is martial and provisional; the enemy is not evil-in-general but the primordial foe recurring; the cosmos is a fortress, not a home.
- Implied values: kingship legitimation (victor-god → king-ideology → royal ritual); calendar war-rites; the sea/outside as a moral category.
- Craft hooks: military service as liturgy; the new-year rite refights the battle (the specific Enūma Eliš/akītu performance link is contested in Assyriology — to-verify, per comparative-frameworks.md §3); diplomacy phrased around "we do not negotiate with the sea"; astronomer-priests as war-planners. Cross-type note: dismemberment (§5b) is the usual sequel — combat makes the victor, dismemberment makes the world.

## 9. Dual-creator pairs

- Three sub-shapes. (a) Adversarial dualism: two co-eternal principles, creation versus counter-creation. (b) Twin culture-heroes: one origin, opposed characters, jointly making. (c) Generative pair or complementarity: production by pairing, ambivalence included.
- Exemplars (a): Zoroastrian — Ohrmazd deliberately creates a good world; Ahriman bursts in from darkness and counter-creates winter and noxious beasts; a 12,000-year cosmic clock runs to the renovation (frashokereti) where evil is destroyed and the world made imperishable (Bundahishn, West 1880; Boyce 1975). (b): Iroquois — twins Sapling and Flint, grandsons of Sky Woman, make and ruin by turns (Parker 1923); Micmac — Gluskap against Malsum (Rand 1894); Maya — the elder diviner pair Xmucane and Xpiyacoc under Heart of Sky, and the Hero Twins whose Xibalba victory re-founds humanity (Tedlock 1985). (c): Izanagi and Izanami, generative until death divides them (Philippi 1968); yin-yang as complementary pairing, explicitly not an adversarial dualism (Wang 2012); trickster co-creators — Raven releases sun, moon, and stars from boxes (Swanton 1909), the Winnebago Hare (Radin 1956), Coyote, Anansi: the junior maker whose errors are how the world got interesting.
- Implied cosmology: (a) the world is contested territory and time is a countdown, so moral categories are cosmic; (b) ambivalence is structural — the same hands that built also ruined; (c) pairs produce; singleness is sterile.
- Implied values: (a) moral clarity, proselytizing zeal, ascetic discipline; (b) tolerance for the flawed world, humor as theology; (c) marriage and complementarity ideology.
- Craft hooks: pair two fictional cultures as mirror dualisms of opposite polarity (one adversarial, one complementary) to generate interfaith conflict; "the evil twin's works" as a class of landscape features; in-world heresy = collapsing the pair or promoting one pole. Calibration: "good twin / evil twin" is often a late layer, sharpened by missionaries or editorial selection in the recording — verify per tradition whether the opposition is emic before reusing it as ancient structure (to-verify per-tradition).

## 10. Cross-cutting: creation by speech — the kotodama complex

- Creation-by-word is not an eighth type; it is a mechanism riding on the others: Ptah's heart-and-tongue (creation through utterance, §7); Genesis's decrees spoken over waters; Popol Vuh's spoken "let it be" (§7); Māori cosmogony recited as whakapapa with karakia (§5); Australian ancestral singing that leaves songs-as-tracks across the land, so that singing is surveying and land-tenure (Munn 1973; Stanner 1953); Japanese kotodama — words as spirit-bearing acts, with the Man'yōshū's "land blessed by the word-spirit" (kotodama no sakiwau kuni; exact poem number to-verify).
- The package a word-creation culture drags along — the language-coupling checklist this lane hands off: a sacred register distinct from the vernacular; name-magic and true names (naming is an act of power over the named — couples naming-systems); taboo lexicons and euphemism ladders (the dangerous word is not said — couples politeness-taboo-euphemism); oath forms as mini-creations (binding speech re-enacts the first speech); blasphemy and defamation treated as cosmic crimes; recitation genres — genealogy, liturgy, riddle-hymn — as high-prestige "literacy" even in oral cultures (couples orality-formulaic-language); puns and folk etymologies as legitimate cosmological argument; a scribal/bard/priest caste owning the creation-text; institutional fear of silence, mis-naming, and translation.
- Design rule: pick which speech act is cosmogonic — decree, song, recited genealogy, whispered true name. The answer determines whether the culture's sacred power looks legal, musical, genealogical, or occult, and which profession holds the top of the prestige ladder.

## 11. Layering rule and application checklist

- Real traditions layer types; the realistic configuration is one dominant type + one secondary layer + one ambient mechanism. Enūma Eliš = parent-union → combat → dismemberment → word (Marduk's command splits her). Māori = recited genealogy-word → parent-separation. Kojiki = annunciation-word → parent-pair → purification as continuing creation. Norse = primordial-cow variant + dismemberment + ongoing combat. Navajo = emergence + spoken blessing (hózhó logic) + failed worlds. Popol Vuh = word + failed creations + elder pair + twins.
- Checklist, in order: (1) Decide what "making" is in this culture (§1 variables) before naming any god. (2) Pick the dominant type and secondary layer; set time-shape. (3) Sacralize the matching profession — diver-type: fishers, messengers; word-type: scribes; combat-type: kingship and the army; emergence-type: guides, midwives; egg-type: astrologers; parent-type: genealogists, elders; dual-type: inquisitors or marriage-priests. (4) Set the calendar: the new-year rite reenacts the cosmogony beat (Eliade's tool; akītu-style performance links are contested — to-verify). (5) Set eschatology from the creation type — combat: final battle and renewal; ex nihilo: linear consummation or new creation; egg: return and emanation cycles; emergence: the next world; dual: renovation; parent: decay or re-separation anxiety; diver: land-loss and flood fear. (6) Set language behavior from §10. (7) Name the geography from the creation act — organs, dive-sites, emergence holes, shell-frontier, battlefield. (8) Assign the origin of evil: who failed at creation? (the trickster's joke, the twin's quarrel, Tiamat's grievance, the wooden people) — this one sentence is the culture's moral system. (9) Stress-test: what does this culture think is wrong with the world, and what would fixing it look like? Both answers must be readable off the creation story; if not, the story is decoration — rewrite it.
- Quick-build sketches: (i) word-first maritime culture — sung creation over waters, scribes hold land-tenure, blasphemy is treason, eschatology is the Last Quiet (the song ending); (ii) emergence mesa culture — four ruined worlds behind, a remediation craft-ethic, the guide-caste owns the calendar, sin is importing an upper-world disease, dread is a fifth climb coming; (iii) dismemberment river empire — the king as executor of the primordial sacrifice, provinces named for organs, tax as recomposing the body, rebellion as hemorrhage, and the victim's name under taboo (§10 coupling).

## 12. Calibration: contested and discredited claims

- Pan-Babylonianism (everything descends from Mesopotamia): rejected. Chaoskampf is a real widely distributed motif cluster; wholesale diffusion claims are not (Gunkel-era excess; Day 1985 is the moderate descendant).
- Solar mythology and "disease of language" (Max Müller's 19th-c. school): long abandoned; do not generate "all dragon myths are sunsets" readings.
- Matriarchal prehistory and the universal Great Goddess (Gimbutas-style readings; the generic "female earth / male sky" typology): rejected by mainstream archaeology and history of religions (Eller 2000; Hutton 1999). Judge each world-parent myth by who acts in the specific text, not by projected gender cosmology.
- Dundes's earth-diver psychoanalysis (primeval waters as mother): a period piece; the geographic distribution data remain valuable (Dundes 1962).
- Jungian archetypes and monomyth-style universals: unfalsifiable; usable as private generation heuristics, never as in-world facts (comparative-frameworks.md §2, §7).
- Eliade's cosmogony-as-paradigm ("every creation repeats the cosmogony"): design language, not ethnographic fact; his Enūma Eliš–akītu performance link is contested in Assyriology (to-verify).
- Witzel's Laurasian mythology (2013) — a single Paleolithic "world novel" reconstructed by comparison: heavily contested; do not use as evidence of common origin or diffusion.
- Genesis 1: ex nihilo versus formation-from-waters is a live scholarly debate; the doctrine's crystallization is Hellenistic-era (May 1994; Levenson 1988) — see §7.
- Pangu: first attested only in the 3rd c. CE (Xu Zheng); Indian Purusha influence is debated (Bodde 1961; Birrell 1993). Do not present Pangu as "ancient Chinese cosmology" in canon notes.
- Kalevala runo 1: Lönnrot's 19th-c. redaction of authentic oral runos — a constructed epic, not received scripture.
- Cushing's Zuni cosmology (1896): a single unreliable narrator; prefer modern Zuni-sourced material (to-verify for a specific recommended edition).
- Dogon cosmic-egg / Sirius material: rests on Griaule's contested ethnography (van Beek 1991); the Sirius B claim is pseudoarchaeology-adjacent — never use.
- "Dreamtime": a Spencer & Gillen (1899) English coinage flattening distinct local concepts (Tjukurpa, Altyerre, Wangarr, Jukurrpa); use local terms, and do not import Chatwin's Songlines (1987) as ethnography.
- "Hopi Fifth-World apocalypse" and the Maya-2012 apocalypticism: modern anglophone inventions rejected by specialists (Geertz 1994; Mayanist consensus).
- Standing comparative rule: matched motif detail, not vibes; type-sharing does not imply historical relatedness (comparative-frameworks.md §7 applies verbatim).
