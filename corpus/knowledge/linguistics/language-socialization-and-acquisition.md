## 9. What a worldbuilder does with this

Use the rows as conditional design prompts, not prescriptions. Select an option, combine alternatives, or specify exceptions for a named setting.


| Worldbuilder choice | Possible consequence for dialogue | Possible consequence for social structure |
|---|---|---|
| Children are corrected openly | Scenes may include explicit repair, evaluation, or public restatement. | Make the correction-giver's authority and the learner's public standing design variables. |
