## 2. The form-building operations — how a substitution is minted

Eight operations, attested exemplars at the granularity recorded in the registering entries. "Ordering power" previews §5: which bracketing tests can act on the form. A minted substitute usually combines two operations; record all of them.

| Op | Shape | Attested exemplars (path:line at HEAD `fd55e1f`) | Ordering power |
|---|---|---|---|
| OP-1 mincing | clip, distort, or rhyme the charged form, preserving a recognizable cue | zounds < God's wounds; sacrébleu < sacré Dieu; chikushō < 畜生 (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:102`; OED/etymonline at `synthesis:17`) | test A (the minced form ages with the word it clips); test C (censor lists, stage editions) |
| OP-2 compound circumlocution → univerbation | a protective description fossilizes into one word whose motivation goes opaque | medvědь "honey-eater", lokys "licker", Mesikämmen "honey-paw" (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:101`) | test A (the compound's members age independently until univerbation); test D (descriptive coinages are its false positives) |
| OP-3 semantic shift | an existing word is repurposed as the polite form, no new material | Eumenides "the kindly ones"; Plouton "the wealthy one" (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:104`) | test D only (semantic-chain bracketing against a registered ladder) — the weakest test, never sole evidence |
| OP-4 borrowing | the substitute is imported from a contact language | conquest meat/animal strata as the *contrast* case (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:103` — strata from conquest, not taboo); traders' animal names generally | test B (younger than the contact event that supplies it); falls under `verification/contact-strata-verification-gate.md` once built |
| OP-5 graphic substitution | the written channel is altered while speech is untouched | imperial bìhuì — a dynasty's documents dated by missing characters, mechanism mainstream, specific title cases thinly documented (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:75`; W22 at `synthesis:8`); written G-d (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:104`) | test C (statutes, editions, scribal rules); never conflated with the spoken channel (DR-TS-04) |

- **Formal vs functional, stated once:** `synthesis/taboo-euphemism-lifecycle.md`'s mechanism table asks *what the taboo does* (replace a label, avoid a name, partition a register); this table asks *what the new form is made of*. Read every candidate through both tables before bracketing: the functional row chooses the channel tests, the formal row chooses the material tests.

## 3. The word-level lifecycle — a branching state graph, not a ladder

States are form-states of one lexical item; transitions are form-events with bracketing hooks. The graph branches at every transition, and no state obliges any other (the corpus's own rule: "S3 can persist without S4a; S4b and S4c can coexist" — `synthesis/taboo-euphemism-lifecycle.md:62`; AB06 and B12 supply no constant rate, `:29`).

| State | Form-state | Bracketing hook |
|---|---|---|
| W0 | the term circulates plain, all channels | pre-taboo stratum; whatever laws act on the plain form act here |
| W1 | charged: at least one channel restricted (say / write / display / hear / quote — the act-in-context split, `synthesis:28`) | the restriction event may be datable (statute, edict, platform rule) or reconstructable only negatively |
| W2 | substituted: one or more substitutes live in the restricted channel(s), minted per §2 ops | each substitute carries its own birth bracketing |
| W3a | conventionalized fossil: the substitute is the ordinary word, motivation opaque | opacity is a *later* condition than adoption (F-TS-04) |
| W3b | live rung: the substitute itself draws the charge (treadmill contamination) | the next substitution starts a new W2 column; rungs coexist by register |
| W4 | replaced: the rung's successor occupies the channel; the old substitute remains as fossil or register doublet | ordering between the rungs is exactly the §5 read-off |
| W5 | reanalyzed: folk etymology, revival, archaizing quotation, factional badge | in-world etymologies are data *about* W5 speakers, never evidence about W0-W2 (DR-TS-07) |

- **Compartmentalization is the normal end-state:** ritual chant, everyday speech, hunters' register, and legal formula can each hold a different state of the same item simultaneously (`politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:104` preserves such a set in attested data; the corpus's design rule `:106` seeds exactly this). A read-off that outputs a single sequence has usually averaged distinct channels — re-run it per channel (DR-TS-04).

## 4. The read-off — what the procedure licenses

Given a lexicon (constructed or attested) and a candidate set of taboo-related forms, the read-off produces a **partial order** of the strata, per channel, with confidence and excluded alternatives. It never produces: a date for the taboo's onset unless an anchor exists; a total order across channels; or an absolute chronology from form shape alone (F-TS-07). Output discipline follows the synthesis entry's reporting rule — intervals plus confidence, or "insufficient evidence", never an invented century (`synthesis/taboo-euphemism-lifecycle.md:156`).

## 5. The bracketing tests

| Test | Logic | Inputs it needs | What it licenses | Primary failure it prevents |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. sound-law bracketing | a form showing a named law's *output* postdates the law's operation; a form preserving the law's *input* in the same environment predates it — the corpus's ordered-laws discipline (C13 via `historical-linguistics.md:3`) | the language's ordered law list; the form's shape at birth | ordering substitutions against each other and against W0; ageing a fossil's material | reading etymological opacity as age (F-TS-04) |
| B. loan bracketing | a borrowed substitute is younger than the contact event that supplies it (borrowing scales, T&T via `language-contact.md:3`); borrowed material cannot antedate its source's availability | the contact history; source-language identification | a terminus post quem for the taboo event in that channel | imported-substitute mistaken for inherited stratum; conquest strata misread as taboo strata (F-TS-02) |
| C. legal/orthographic bracketing | a statute, censor list, edition state, or scribal rule is a dated observable in one channel (Rosa's Law's implementation clauses as the mechanism exemplar, `synthesis:139-144`; bìhuì's missing characters, `politeness:75`) | the legal/editorial record; the channel split | terminus post/ante quem for the *rule*, bounded exactly as the synthesis entry bounds it (`:153` — a mandatory form constrains a compliant copy, not every utterance) | rule-declaration date confused with rule-enforcement or form-birth dates (`synthesis:65`) |
| D. semantic-chain bracketing | position against a registered ladder constrains only relative order within that ladder's speech community (the ladders at `politeness-taboo-euphemism.md:84-92`) | a registered ladder; community match | weak relative ordering; calibration of churn speed per motor (`:82`) | ladder position treated as an absolute clock (F-TS-07); ladder borrowed across communities |

- **Precedence and sufficiency:** A and B are material tests — each can date a form against the language's own history independently. C is a record test — it dates rules and observable channel events. D is calibration only and must never stand alone. Two independent tests agreeing is the corpus's own bar for coupled claims; one test is a hypothesis, never a verdict.

## 6. Negative strata and channel silence

- A restriction can leave **no lexical trace** in its channel: OP-6's spoken substitution leaves the written form intact and the spoken record silent in most archives (`synthesis:122` — loss of an utterance opportunity need not erase a written record); an intention-based rule can be violated by a minced form without any surviving record of the violation (`synthesis:73`). Record the silence as a **negative stratum** with its channel, its archive basis, and the search that was made — a negative stratum is a result that bounds later claims, not a gap in the method (DR-TS-06).
- Survival bias runs through every read: officials preserve sanctioned forms, court records preserve condemned ones, collectors preserve conspicuous ones (`synthesis:94`). State which archive class each stratum was read from.

|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Applying it — build order for a constructed lexicon, and a demonstration

1. Seed the taboo's **channels** and sanction owner (the social apparatus is `politeness-taboo-euphemism.md` §3's job — this entry consumes it).
2. Mint one or more **substitutes per restricted channel**, each with: operation(s) from §2, the mechanism row from synthesis §2, and the state it starts at (W2).
3. Assign **ordering hooks**: which named sound laws act *after* the substitute's birth (test A); which contact event could have supplied it, if borrowed (test B); which statute/edition/platform rule anchors its channel (test C); which registered ladder calibrates it, if any (test D).
4. Decide endpoints per channel: fossil (W3a), live rung (W3b → W4), or reanalysis (W5) — at least one channel keeps a **stable endpoint** instead of renewing every ladder (`synthesis:185`).
5. Emit lexicon rows carrying: form, op class, channel, state, bracketing hooks, competing explanations considered and excluded, negative strata.
