## Remit: consistency is scoped compatibility, not cultural uniformity
- Run on a supplied candidate bundle or a read-only world-bible snapshot requested by lore-shokunin; this entry defines a method, not an audit of any existing fictional world.
- A table is a verifier of a **declared design decision**, not a license to impose an Earth analogue. Statistical tendencies, historical examples, designer quotas, and physical constraints have different evidential force.
- Compare assertions only after matching culture/community, locality, era interval, social position, medium, and knowledge-holder. Two rival cults assigning different meanings to one color need not contradict each other.
- Distinguish **world-state proposal**, **institutional rule**, **reported practice**, **in-world belief**, and **designer analogy**. A sacred story, an edict, and a surviving garment do not assert the same kind of fact.
- A rule that says names bind souls does not establish that names actually have magical effects; a grammatical evidential does not certify truth. Both questions remain with lore-shokunin.
- Do not flatten multilingual polities, diasporas, minority cults, rural districts, oral communities, extinct languages, or occupational groups into one national template.
- A reference civilization must be bounded by locality and period and documented across language family + writing, myth/pantheon, symbols, era model, costume/materials, and naming practices; do not borrow only its visually attractive parts.
- All results below are **provisional reference findings**. Even a locally compatible bundle is neither canon-approved nor a replacement for lore-shokunin's own verification.

## Run procedure and finding vocabulary
1. Freeze the input revision/version, recipient, requested scope, and coverage roster; enumerate culture–era units and their known contact edges. Include missing and unavailable inputs rather than silently reducing scope.
2. Give claims stable local IDs and source locations. Record `{claim, kind, community, locality, valid-from/to, language-stage, register, medium, evidence, uncertainty}`; separate an artifact's manufacture, use, deposition, and modern recording dates.
3. Expand each culture–era dossier into the coupled systems using C01; run applicable rows over the declared roster and record reasons for exclusions. Sampling produces a sampled result, never a whole-world clearance.
4. For each row, retain `{check-ID, corpus-table IDs + row, input pointers, action performed, observed result, status, alternative explanations, affected dependencies, reviewer}`. An action merely proposed has not been performed.
5. Re-run direct dependents after a change, then follow transitive edges until no newly affected claims remain. Preserve the old reading and the proposed replacement; do not silently reconcile contradictions.
6. Send the findings, unresolved questions, and noncanonical repair options to lore-shokunin. Stop at handoff; do not select the official variant, rewrite history, or edit the bible.

| Status | Meaning in this method | Permitted next move |
|---|---|---|
| COMPATIBLE | the recorded action found no conflict within its stated input/scope | submit evidence to lore; do not expand the claim to unseen material |
| CONFLICT | mutually incompatible explicit specifications share the same scope, or a stipulated dependency demonstrably fails | describe both assertions and candidate repairs; lore rules |
| VARIANT | differences have explicit regional, institutional, genre, or temporal scope | retain both and their boundaries; request a lore ruling only where scopes overlap |
| OPEN | evidence, a needed parameter, an executable derivation, or source verification is missing | mark the affected claim `(to-verify)` and name what would settle it |
| OUT-OF-SCOPE | the recipient's declared scope excludes the item, with a recorded reason | retain the exclusion in coverage; never count it as a passed check |

## Calibration, thin coverage, and provenance cautions
- **Framework status:** Joseph Campbell's monomyth, Jungian symbol dictionaries, Frazerian universal religion-stages, and pan-cultural color meanings do not supply established universal laws. T15/T23/T24 are bounded design aids; the particular scholarship and each tradition still require evaluation.
- **Tendencies are not entailments:** morphology does not determine word order; kin vocabulary does not determine marriage law; script type does not determine intelligence; a word's absence does not establish a conceptual incapacity. Read T02/T03/T04/T11 with their calibration, not as deterministic validators.
- **Corpus conventions are not external scholarship:** primary-domain/satellite limits, a shared register partition, grounding quotas, exemplar counts, or prescribed scene numbers are local craft policies. Record whether the candidate bundle adopts them; do not fail a culture merely for being polysemous, uneven, or unlike a table's preferred example.
- **Material analogies are defeasible:** dyes do not set an absolute ceiling on color vocabulary; panel construction permits widths beyond a loom; animals and images travel; garments and terms outlive industries. T25/T26/T41 require acquisition, assembly, transmission, or survival explanations, not a one-to-one ecology–meaning map.
- **Chronicles can be scarce or late:** distinguish proposed event date, earliest surviving attestation, composition, manuscript copy, translation, and collection date. Do not backdate a rite, precise costume rule, or complete pantheon from a late witness without supporting evidence `(to-verify)`.
- **Colonial and elite-source bias:** identify collector/translator, elicitation setting, language competence, patron, hostile agenda, coercion, and excluded voices where known. Multiple summaries repeating one collector are not independent confirmation; sparse chronicles do not imply sparse intellectual or ritual life.
- **Thin knowledge here:** this checklist does not independently verify tradition-specific Indigenous, African, Asian, or other local ceremonial interpretations embedded in the comparative tables. Such particulars, present-day community protocols, and source-language readings remain `(to-verify)` unless documented for the exact practice; do not invent a pan-regional substitute.
- **Open questions stay open:** uncertainty is claim-specific. A verified book title does not verify every example quoted from it; a documented practice does not license generalization to a whole civilization, gender, language family, or era.

## Checkable editions and what a worldbuilder can take
- Campbell (2013), 3rd ed.: [MIT Press edition record, ISBN 9780262518499](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262518499/historical-linguistics-third-edition/). Use comparative derivation and relative chronology; do not turn vocabulary resemblance into an absolute clock.
- Hymes (1974): [University of Pennsylvania Press edition record](https://www.pennpress.org/9780812210651/foundations-in-sociolinguistics/). Use speech-event participants, purpose, medium, norms, and genre to scope a scene; do not read a grammar apart from its users.
- Douglas (1966): [contemporary bibliographic review record, P. H. Gulliver (1967), *Bulletin of SOAS*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-school-of-oriental-and-african-studies/article/mary-douglas-purity-and-danger-an-analysis-of-concepts-of-pollution-and-taboo-viii-188-pp-london-routledge-and-kegan-paul-1966-25s/D55A0F3DBBDAE18B96D003B507E9AE16). Use classification/boundary questions as a design lens, not a universally correct explanation of each food or purity rule.
- Wylie (2002): [University of Washington bibliographic record and summary](https://phil.washington.edu/research/books/thinking-things-essays-philosophy-archaeology). Use explicit inferential bridges and alternative explanations; elegance of a reconstructed system is not evidence that it existed.
- These links establish checkable works/editions and limited summaries, not a fresh page-by-page verification of their contents. Exact quotations, disputed historical particulars, and inherited corpus examples must be located in the underlying sources before being promoted from `(to-verify)`.
- **What a worldbuilder can take:** a coverage roster, era-scoped claim graph, named verifier for every check, a reproducible language/ritual/material dependency trail, and bounded alternatives that preserve cultural disagreement. **What must not be taken:** a universal civilization template, permission to reuse restricted cultural knowledge, or authority to decide canon; those questions are not settled by internal consistency, and canon rulings are explicitly deferred to lore-shokunin.
