## 8. Calibration, thin evidence, and provenance cautions
| Claim or shortcut | Calibration status | Required handling |
|---|---|---|
| IPA notation and glossing conventions | established descriptive tools, not a proof of analysis | justify contrasts, morphemes, and category choices separately |
| WALS word-order or syllable categories | sample-based summaries, not universal packages | read chapter definitions and underlying grammars; record dataset version if using counts |
| “300 words makes a language” | project-budget claim only; rejected as a completeness test | test connected speech; expand when scenes require it |
| Universal politeness/face ladders | contested scholarship: the universality of “face” is itself disputed (Matsumoto 1988); detailed reception beyond this source set | no ranking of whole peoples as more/less polite; consult `../linguistics/politeness-taboo-euphemism.md` before a historical comparison |
| Sounds imply temperament; vocabulary absence implies conceptual inability | unsupported inference, not licensed by this method | choose aesthetic effects as DESIGN; permit paraphrase and variation |
| Court chronicle represents everyone's speech and dress | source-biased generalization | identify patron, author, date, genre, preservation, translation, and omitted groups; sparse evidence stays sparse |
| Colonial transcription or outsider ritual account | potentially coercive, translation-mediated, and selective evidence | distinguish reported wording from outsider analysis; seek community-authored work and record disagreements |
- This template does not establish expertise in any particular sacred-language tradition. Restricted or living ritual vocabulary needs tradition-specific research and appropriate access; analogy is not permission to reproduce it.
- Evidence docket: claim [ ]; author/year/edition/page or stable record [ ]; quotation versus paraphrase [ ]; attested/reconstructed/contested [ ]; colonial/elite bias [ ]; knowledge thin here [ ]; chronicle evidence scarce here [ ]; `(to-verify)` task and consequence [ ].
- Gate G8: [ ] every real-world assertion has a checkable source or inline `(to-verify)`; unresolved reference claims do not silently become constraints or canon; DESIGN choices are labeled rather than disguised as historical facts.
