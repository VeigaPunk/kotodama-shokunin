## §2 The build order: world-view, then progression, then the expression of the progression

- The three steps and their order are stated as a sequence he followed: 「整个过程里，我先做的是世界观，再考虑怎么升级，再考虑升级的表现。」 (loc: P68, under the subheading 确定风格、设定世界、推导故事) (S883).
- The order is a dependency order, not a plan finished before writing began: 「一开始其实不是所有设定都想好了。」 (loc: P69) (S883).
- He describes the pipeline as iterative, with candidates pruned as the derivation proceeds: 「是通过一次次的尝试、推导确定下来的。」 (loc: P69) (S883).
- His account ends in one surviving path, which is his characterization of his own search rather than a claim that only one path exists: 「最后找到一条合适的路径，那就是唯一的答案。」 (loc: P69) (S883).
- A mechanic is rejected when it conflicts with the method the world-view already fixed: 「既然有了扮演法，那么晋升的过程就不能采用吸收灵气、冥想这种传统的方式。」 (loc: P66) (S883).
- The third step is a fit to the world-view's tone, decided after the laws exist: 「服食魔药和扮演法是符合世界观调性的升级方式」 (loc: P68) (S883).
- The earlier interview states the same order on its own: the setting was already settled before the power system was designed 「首先想的是，我的背景设定既然已经明确了」, and the ladder was then built from the chosen register 「然后根据塔罗牌做一个力量体系设定」 (loc: para 51) (S884).

## §3 The derivation rule: premise, inference, material carrier, laws, mechanics

- The premise is the world's origin: 「万事万物都从最初造物主而来」 (loc: P68) (S883).
- The step from premise to power is an inference he states as an inference: 「“所有的非凡力量也从他而来”是个合理的推论」 (loc: P68) (S883).
- The inference is required to take a material form, and that form yields the laws, in one sentence: 「这个推论要有物质性的表现，然后就有了非凡特性，并推导出一系列非凡特性定律。」 (loc: P68) (S883).
- The laws then decide the mechanics, chosen to fit them rather than assumed and justified afterwards: 「有了这些定律，我就觉得，服食魔药和扮演法是符合世界观调性的升级方式。」 (loc: P68) (S883).
- The same shape runs at a smaller scale, top-down from a chosen register: he takes the twenty-two major cards 「我先把22张大牌拿来，看哪些可以用，哪些需要修改。」 (loc: P61) (S883), then derives the ladder from them 「然后从上往下推导出整个序列途径。」 (loc: P61) (S883).
- The realism of a ladder is not uniform: it is carried at the low ranks 「越贴近现实就越有代入感」 (loc: P62) (S883), and thinned at the top where a plain occupation breaks the fiction 「等级很高以后，再用现实职业会很违和」 (loc: P62) (S883).
- The aim he states for the whole construction: 「贴近现实、来源于现实、又能在现实中展现出神秘学魅力」 (loc: P62) (S883).

Read as a rule (this entry's formulation, not the author's): fix the world's premise first, derive the world's laws from it, and let those laws choose the ladder — a ladder that has to be exempted from the premise is the signal that the step was taken in the wrong order.

## §4 One practitioner's stated tolerance, on a single tier-C row

What follows is what one author says he tolerates as a reader and does as a writer, at paras 32–35 of one magazine interview. It is a lead for designing a disclosure plan; it is not a rule of serial fiction, and nothing else in either row supports it.

- The first failure mode is a first chapter that dumps the system: 「它的第一章就是抛设定，把世界观、整个修炼体系抛给你。」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- The second is a dump that continues past the opening: 「但如果一开始前面两三章都是设定，那我大概是不会看下去了」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- The opposite failure is a world kept unclear: 「很多一开始设定不明确的小说我也是看不下去的」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- The condition precedes the schedule in his sentence: 「如果开头足够有趣，有钩子，有悬念」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- What is released, and how: 「那么你把整个世界观和设定逐渐地抛入」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- When, in his own words and with his own hedge: 「把它放在前面大概20章之内慢慢来，我觉得是问题不大的。」 (loc: para 35) (S884).
- On his own book the mechanism is a suspense hook first, then staged release: 「用悬念作钩子吸引人读下去」 (loc: para 33) (S884), and 「然后在整个故事的推进中一点点地把设定给抛出来」 (loc: para 33) (S884).
- His reason for not front-loading is reader-friendliness, stated as a comparison between two versions of the same book: 「如果我一开始就把这个设定给你们，肯定是比现在这个版本更不友好」 (loc: para 33) (S884).
- The constraint he names is a reader's tolerance, and he grounds it in his own reading rather than a measurement: 「我对不同的小说有不同的容忍度」 (loc: para 35) (S884).

The bound is his tolerance, not his book's schedule: the interviewer notes that readers of 《诡秘之主》 understand the setting only 「到几十章后」 (loc: para 32) — dozens of chapters, not twenty (S884).

## §5 What to do with it

The three steps as a drafting gate list (S883):

| step | the question it must answer before the next step opens | failure sign in a draft |
|---|---|---|
| world-view | can the world's origin premise be stated in one sentence, and does every later law trace back to it? | a mechanic that needs an exception to the premise |
| progression | do the world's own laws produce this ladder, and does the ladder's register fit the world's tone? | a ladder imported from another genre and dressed in local names |
| expression | does the mechanic exhibit the law (a material cost, a symptom, a rite) rather than merely label a rank? | ranks that change nothing visible and cost nothing |

The disclosure question as three prompts, offered on one practitioner's say-so and not as a test anyone passes (S884; the third is this entry's extension of 逐渐地):

| design question | what the reported practice suggests | what it warns against |
|---|---|---|
| does the opening run on a hook rather than on the system? | a first scene interesting on its own terms, with 有钩子、有悬念 named as the condition | a first chapter that explains the world |
| is the world's information delivered inside roughly the first twenty chapters, if the hook holds? | rules arrive in instalments while the story moves | chapters two and three still exposition |
| is each released rule attached to a scene that needs it? | the reader learns a rule by watching it operate | a rule stated where nothing yet depends on it |

Worked example, hypothetical and canon-free: take a premise "the dead answer, at a price paid in memory". The laws step has to state what is taken and who enforces it; the expression step has to show a consultation where the price is paid and seen. Then the disclosure step asks which of those facts the first scene needs — and the ladder of ranks or rites is written last, as the consequence the laws already implied. Marked as an illustration of the sequence, not a template for any world.

## §6 What this amendment does not establish, and the handoff

- Nothing here measures readers. The tolerance is the author's own, from his own reading, and is recorded as such; a different audience is neither evidence against it nor evidence for it.
- The disclosure material rests on one tier-C row and is a lead — useful for designing a disclosure plan, never quotable as a rule of serial fiction; the row's own re-quote is what a later round must re-open before strengthening it.
- The twenty-chapter figure is his 大概, in a sentence he closes with 「我觉得是问题不大的。」 (loc: para 35) (S884); nothing in either row supports a hard boundary at twenty or at any other number.
- No second author's build order is compared here, so the order is not shown to be typical — only to be one working method, stated twice by the same author about different books.
- Handoff: the derived ladder, the laws behind it and any disclosure plan are records for a world bible, which `synthesis/worldbible-integration-contract.md` hands to lore; this entry ends at that handoff and records no canon.
