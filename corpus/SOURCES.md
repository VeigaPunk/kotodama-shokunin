# SOURCES — where kotodama learns

The lane's curated reading list and the standing record of its taste:
what each source teaches, how far to trust it for which scope, and when to
look again. Shape and rules: fleet contract `SOURCES-CONTRACT-v1`
(kaika, 2026-10-19). Claim admission stays in `QUALITY.md`; the research
method stays chousa's. Every row was opened in the round that wrote it —
the round lesson's `## Gate` (`lessons/round-49.md` for S001–S043) holds
each row's URL, access date and verbatim quote. Append-only: only
`status`, `recheck` and `used-by` change in place, each change logged
old→new in a round lesson; a correction is a new row whose `teaches`
opens `supersedes S0NN —`.

Tiers are scoped to the row's `scope`, never global. **A** — primary or
authoritative for that scope (spec, official docs, source code, original
paper, maintainer statement, the lane's own re-runnable gate output).
**B** — strong secondary (expert write-up with reproducible detail, survey,
textbook). **C** — lead only, never sole support for an admitted entry.
`community` is never A. Tier is curation, not truth. Status: `queued`
(opened, worth mining) · `mined` (cited by the paths in `used-by`) ·
`rejected` (kept with reason and flip — a rejection is evidence of taste).

Census (from the lane root): the seven commands in the contract's
`## Census` block; `python3 reports/round-49/run-validation.py` runs them.

## Paths

- Typology evidence chain — S001 → S002 → S003 → S004: name the languoid in
  Glottolog first (it fixes identity, family and the glottocode every other
  dataset keys on), then take feature proportions from WALS, then check segment
  plausibility against PHOIBLE inventories, then Grambank for grammatical design
  space beyond what WALS covers.
- Kinship and society grounding — S007 → S005: read D-PLACE codings for the
  society's stated regime first (tagged by date and source), then Kinbank for the
  actual term inventories and its worked null-result caution before asserting
  any marriage–terminology coupling.
- Lexical history design — S006 → S011 → S010: mine DatSemShift for attested
  meaning-change directions, sanity-check sound-change steps against the Index
  Diachronica's cited sources (never the index alone), then replay the whole
  staged history deterministically in Lexurgy.
- Conlang method — S008 → S009 → S012: learn the craft order from the LCK, then
  consult CALS only as a census of what other conlangers choose (community tier),
  then ConlangCrafter to see what staged LLM pipelines now automate and where
  their evaluation is still thin.
- Motif-grid grounding — S013 → S014 → S015: anchor motif identities in the
