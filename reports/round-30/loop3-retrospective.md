### Concrete operator recommendation — prospective two-channel contract

**Do not lower 95% to fit the historical 77–78%, and do not apply a quiet-tree
raw-hash gate during a moving build. Approve a split instrument prospectively.**

1. **F-replay, evaluated only after settle.** Freeze the complete command/input/
   tool/dependency closure and exact baseline/evaluation revisions, runner hash,
   cwd and environment. Commit all prerequisites, including formerly ignored
   logs or scratch inputs that are necessary to reproduce the result. Run only
   in disposable, truly isolated trees; verify that a runner cannot synchronize
   live inputs into the historical tree or overwrite filed receipts.
2. **Keep ≥95% as the raw-byte reproducibility floor for a new pilot**, with
   **all preregistered command rows as the headline denominator**. Require 100%
   attempted/accounted coverage; missing, non-executable or unevaluable rows
   remain in the denominator and never become stable successes. Also publish
   the comparable-only ratio, no-hash count and carried count, separately.
   Compare stdout, stderr **and exit**, not stdout alone. All nonmatching rows
   require a disposition; dispositions do not increase the stable numerator.
3. **Require zero unexplained semantic regressions and zero protected-byte
   mutations**, even if the raw ratio is above 95%. Semantic equivalence must
   preserve exact finding IDs, severity, locations, details and expected-set
   membership, plus exit status. The 5% byte allowance is not a budget for
   dropping holds, losing law force, accepting malformed input or changing a
   rights/canon boundary. Retain original raw streams. Any allowed temp-root or
   timing normalization must be field-specific, declared before execution and
   shown not to erase a meaningful finding or failure.
4. **F-build/change accounting stays a separate channel.** For each authorized
   source/tool/schema change, require a before/after causal disposition and a
   control. New pins are new rows with explicit supersession. Freeze downstream
   v3 baselines only after the D landing settles. Publish raw change counts;
