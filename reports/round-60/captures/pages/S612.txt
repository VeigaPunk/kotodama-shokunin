Amulet case
Talisman holder
generic title
Silver amulet holders, often containing symbolic material or a piece of paper with a magical or holy inscription, were worn throughout the Muslim world.  They were believed to protect the wearer from all kinds of physical and spiritual dangers.

This amulet case probably contains a tiny copy of the Koran.  It is dated AH 1240 (1825), and has a red cover, which can be seen through the glass opening on the front.  It may have been worn on the upper arm, fastened by straps which would have passed through the loops at either side. 

Rectangular silver amulet case containing a miniature book.  The case has repousse floral designs on all sides, except the base.  There is a round glass window on the lid, showing the inscription on the cover of the book inside.  The lid is hinged to the case, and fastened at one side by a small hook which slides through a loop.  There are two further loops, one on each side, for attachment.  The book inside is written in Arabic characters on paper, and has a red cover.
