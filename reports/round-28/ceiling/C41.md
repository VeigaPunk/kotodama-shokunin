## 0. Headline

**The ceiling is real; one of its five cited members was miscounted, and the
miscount is the filed reader's.** Round 27 filed the arithmetic as **18 legal /
9 illegal** with the abstract class **0 legal / 5 illegal**
(`reports/round-27/law/design.md` §3.2). Re-measured at lane HEAD `69cecbc`
against the delivered packet and the delivered gate, the reading is **19 legal /
8 illegal** and the abstract class is **1 legal / 4 illegal**. The delta is
exactly one row, `L-45 tikmes → semkit`.

The filed 18/9 reproduces **exactly** under the syllabification that never splits
a consonant run — the scan round 27's D seat replaced mid-round
(`5b5abe78…` → `d7a3ad1a…` → `b5406bcd…`). So the filed count is not wrong for
its stamp; it is **superseded by a reader the same round replaced**, and it was
never re-taken afterwards.

Round 28 also measured the **cause**, which round 27 stated as a fact about the
lexicon:

```
reverse is legal  <=>  the base's initial consonant is a licensed coda    27/27
```

The template `(C)V(V)(C)` has 14 onset consonants and only 8 licensed codas, so
the six consonants that are onsets but never codas (`b g h j p w`) are exactly
the refusals. The ceiling is arithmetic over the syllable template — adding
coinages cannot move it, which is the single most important design consequence.

## 3. The cross-tab, and what changes

Round 27's table used **three** classes (`material`, `abstract`, `other`); this
instrument uses **two** (`abstract` = the five named IDs; `material/other` =
everything else). The reconciliation is therefore exact and worth stating:

| class | filed (round 27) | delivered (round 28) | agrees? |

| material + other | 8+10 = **18 legal**, 2+2 = **4 illegal** | **18 legal, 4 illegal** | ✅ **exactly** |

**The divergence is confined to the abstract class and to one member of it.** The
non-abstract 22 words reconcile exactly between the two readers; the whole
discrepancy is `L-45 tikmes` moving from illegal to legal. That is a narrower and
more useful statement than "the count changed": it says the pre-fix reader was
wrong about **one compound**, and right about the other 26 words.

What **reproduces** from round 27's narrative is the part the design rests on:
four of the five legal/institutional abstractions refuse `reverse`, including
`L-30 gis` (the oath itself, `sig` illegal), and the eight landed register forms
are 8-of-8 material-presence bases (re-measured: `tik mol tum nes hum mes rek
sam`, zero abstractions).

**Verdict on the ceiling.** The ceiling stands, and its sharpest case is
unaffected: **the register cannot say "oath" in itself.** The correction narrows
the claim from "all five abstractions refuse" to "four of five refuse, and the
one that does not is a compound that got lucky with its final consonant" — a
narrower claim that is *more* defensible, and which points at the mechanism in §4.

## 4. The mechanism (new this round)

Round 27 stated the ceiling as a fact about a word list. Round 28 measured the
cause, and it is one consonant position:

```
$ python3 -B reports/round-28/ceiling/measure-ceiling.py
mechanism: reverse is legal <=> the base-initial consonant is a licensed coda
           -> True (27/27)
  onsets=14 codas=8 never-coda=b,g,h,j,p,w
```

Sources, both in the delivered component `examples/culture-d4/components/02-phonology.yml`:
the 14-consonant inventory at `:61-75`, the template `(C)V(V)(C)` at `:82`,
`onset_clusters: false` at `:92`, and the eight codas (`n s k l r t d m`) at
`:119`. A reversal moves the base-initial consonant to final position, where it
must be a coda; a base may therefore be reversed **iff** it begins with one of the
eight. Verified 27/27 with no counterexample, and the six never-coda consonants
are exactly the refusals.

**Why this is the load-bearing finding, not a footnote.** It changes the
disposition. If the ceiling were a property of the word list, more coinages would
close it. Because it is arithmetic over the syllable template, no coinage closes
it — only changing the operation or the base list does. That is what makes the
round-26 refusal to add "an escape hatch for abstractions" a *design decision*
rather than an oversight, and it is what the new entry's dispositions (§5) rest
on.
