## 4. Why an amendment and not an edit

Two reasons, both structural rather than stylistic:

1. **The entry's own clause.** §10's append-only discipline binds this entry, and
   the round-27 entry was admitted as filed evidence. Editing its round-27 numbers
   in place would silently rewrite a filed reading that other round-27 artifacts
   cite (`reports/round-27/entry/C45.md` quotes `RSA-4`'s evidence as "measured
   (`0 legal / 5 illegal` over institutional abstractions)"). That citation must
   keep resolving to what it said.
2. **The divergence is the finding.** The filed 18/9 is not noise; it is the
   pre-fix reader's correct output. A rewrite would delete the evidence that a
   legality count is only as trustworthy as its reader — which is `RLC-4` in the
   new entry, and the reason this lane now re-takes such counts after a reader
   changes.

**What a later round must do.** If a further round changes
`tools/gates/argot-derivation-gate.py`'s `syllabify`/`is_legal_syllable`, the
count moves again; the correction is another new section (§14, …) or a new entry
linked from `recheck` — never an edit of §13. That is stated in §13.5's first
limit and in the new entry's `recheck:` line.
