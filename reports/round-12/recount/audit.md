## Finding and limits

The independent sample has 13 distinct command lines from the ledger; 12 reproduce the ledger's `observed` values. The sole mismatch is C23-F1: the round-9 battery command's fresh normalized output hash differs from the ledger's already-dispositioned observed hash, although all structured results and exit codes reproduce. Two raw re-runs gave different hashes, and the local scratch-token leakage explains why normalization did not stabilize them. This is evidence of an environmental reproducibility defect in the hash surface, not a battery count or verdict change.

The one unsuccessful exploratory invocation `python3 tools/source-audit-inventory.py --json --root --summary` (2026-10-17) is excluded from the sample: argparse reports that `--root` requires a value.
