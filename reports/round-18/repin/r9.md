## 7. Limits

- A new revision pins **bytes and a verdict**: it says the frozen copy, under the
  pinned gate revision, produced the recorded exit and finding-ID set. It is not a
  design PASS (four of the seven verdicts are red findings verdicts), not a canon
  ruling, and not a freshness or rights statement.
- `r18-post` pins the *round-18 working tree* bytes. If a later round edits a
  graded gate or input, that later phase needs another revision id — the pinned
  copy must not be rewritten to match a newer run.
- The seven new pins are discovered by the battery's default roots
  (`tools/gates/expected`, `tools/gates/frozen`, `reports/round-9/baselines`),
  which is why `baselines_found` moved 8 → 15. That count is a coverage reading,
  not a quality reading.
- `run.log` files match the lane's `.gitignore` `*.log` pattern; committing this
  revision needs `git add -f` (same note the round-9 and round-11 records carry).
